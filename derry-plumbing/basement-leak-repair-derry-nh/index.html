    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Derry

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. Add a battery backup if storms knock out power.

    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Derry

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It usually ends outside, well away from the foundation, at a spot your local rules allow.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Derry, NH

    Water pressing against the outside of a basement wall works into the weakest spot, usually the cove joint where wall meets floor.     Waterproofing for Older New Hampshire Homes in Derry, NH

    Older basements were often built with block walls and no exterior membrane.
