    What Our Derry, NH Drain Techs Clean

    Bathroom Sink, Tub and Shower Drains in Derry, NH

    Toothpaste and soap scum glue hair into a stringy plug. The pop-up linkage is usually the snag point. We pull the stopper, clean the trap and send a small cable down the branch line toward the stack, and if the stopper keeps catching hair we can adjust it or swap it for one with a simpler design. Usually under an hour.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning in Derry, NH

    Restaurants, salons and offices load drains harder than homes do. A busy shop puts a year of household waste down a line in a month. We bring heavier sectional machines and jetters that handle 4 and 6 inch lines, and we can schedule work before opening or after close so you do not lose a lunch rush to a plumber in the kitchen.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Cleaning in Derry

    Coffee grounds and eggshells are a sink's worst enemies. Both sink to the bottom of the pipe and pack down hard. Our tech feeds a cable through the trap arm and works the clog until the line takes full flow, then checks the disposal and the dishwasher connection, since either one can push food into the line. A standard sink clog is usually under an hour.

    Tub and Shower Drain Cleaning in Derry, NH

    Showers clog slowly, then all at once. A hair mat forms right under the drain cover. We pull the strainer or cable through the tub overflow so the finish does not get scratched, and we clear the trap and the branch beyond it, then run the shower for a few minutes to confirm it drains at full speed with no gurgle.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Cleaning in Derry, NH

    Washers push lint and softener residue into the standpipe. That gunk builds until water spills onto the floor. We clean the standpipe and the branch line, then check that the trap and vent are working properly, and garage floor drains full of grit and gravel get cleared and flushed so they can take a full washer discharge without backing up.

    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Derry

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. A cable punches a hole, while a jetter cleans the pipe back to near its original diameter. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Outdoor & Storm Drain Cleaning in Derry

    Yard drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and roots. Nor'easters and tropical storm remnants can bring heavy rain that overwhelms drainage and floods low-lying areas. Standing water near the foundation is the warning sign. The pipe gets cleaned all the way to the daylight outlet, and the catch basin gets emptied, so the next storm runs off instead of soaking into the soil next to the house.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Derry, NH

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. Jetting strips grease and scale off the walls and carries it out of the line, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.
