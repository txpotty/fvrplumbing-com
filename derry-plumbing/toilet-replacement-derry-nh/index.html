    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Derry, NH

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs.     Commercial Toilet Replacement in Derry, NH

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank.     High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Derry, NH

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more.     Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Derry, NH

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.
