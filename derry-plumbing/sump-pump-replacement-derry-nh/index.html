    Sump Pump Types We Replace in Derry, NH

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Derry

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Figure on an hour or two for a typical swap. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Derry, NH

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. Narrow pits, under about 12 inches across, are where they make the most sense.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Derry, NH

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Derry

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. That is not a sump pump job. We replace the ejector and its discharge check valve, reseal the lid gasket, and confirm the vent ties in. A bad seal shows up as sewer odor.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Derry, NH

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Derry

    A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are smart protection for any finished basement. A standard pump just sits there once the power drops. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. The alarm will tell you which one is running.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Derry

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. The rest is simple arithmetic. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Derry, NH

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. If you have a Zoeller now, we can usually match the discharge outlet and switch style so the swap goes quickly. Pump not keeping up? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.
