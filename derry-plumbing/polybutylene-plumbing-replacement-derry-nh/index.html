    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Derry, NH

    Some homeowners in Derry, NH want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, and join it with lead-free solder or press fittings. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Derry, NH

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance, and most homes are completed in two to four working days with water back on each night.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Derry

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. Plan on a higher price. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Derry, NH

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. New PEX gets sized for the home and strapped to the frame. We check underneath. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, camps and seasonal homes are especially likely to freeze when the heat is turned down. We inspect heat tape and insulation on any exposed pipe and replace what is worn, so the new lines stay protected.
