    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Derry, NH

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one.     Water Heater Inspections in Derry

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Derry

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. Every unit gets its own notes.     Hard Water Damage Assessment in Derry

    Water in much of New Hampshire is soft and often slightly acidic, which can gradually wear down copper pipe and cause pinhole leaks. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout.     Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Derry, NH
