    Propane Water Heater Repair in Derry

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. Propane units need a matching orifice and a control set near 11 inches of water column. Natural gas runs closer to 7 inches, so a heater converted badly, or a regulator that drifted, gives you soot, a yellow flame and a pilot that keeps going out for no obvious reason. A manometer reading tells us the supply pressure. Wrong parts get swapped for the right ones.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Derry

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. The usual failures are the blower motor, the pressure switch and the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. The control's error code is our starting point. Then each part gets a meter test.

    Pilot Light and Ignition Service in Derry

    No hot water and a dark pilot window usually point to the ignition side of the heater. We pull the pilot assembly and look at the thermocouple tip, the pilot orifice and the piezo igniter wire.     Gas Water Heater Noise Diagnosis in Derry

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water speeds that up, and so does a tank that has never been flushed. A high whistle is different. That usually means a relief valve or a partly closed supply valve.
