    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Derry, NH

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Derry, NH

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Derry, NH

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. We size outdoor lines for the full load. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    After-Hours Gas Line Repair in Derry

    Gas problems do not wait for business hours, and losing heat or hot water overnight is a real problem. We answer late calls. Once everyone is out and the gas company has secured things, we send a plumber to find the leak, fix it and test the system. We coordinate with the utility on the meter.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Derry

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Derry building officials. We pull the permit, book the inspection and meet the inspector at the gauge so you do not have to. Inspection usually happens within a few days. After it passes, the gas company can restore service, and we can return to relight the pilots.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Derry, NH

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. It takes about an hour. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any finished basement.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Derry, NH

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Derry, NH

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Derry, NH

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Derry, NH. Inspection covers sizing, approved materials, pipe supports, shutoffs, drip legs and a witnessed or documented pressure test. The permit is on us. A permitted repair also gives you a paper trail for insurance and for any future sale.
