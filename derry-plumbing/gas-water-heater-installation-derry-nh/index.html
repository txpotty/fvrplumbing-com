    Proper Sizing and Venting in Derry, NH

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We figure your household peak demand from bathrooms and people, then make sure the vent diameter, rise and total length match the manufacturer table and local code. Undersized vents spill CO. Draft gets tested on every job.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Derry

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. We inspect the existing vent. We replace bad sections.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Derry, NH

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. A 120-volt outlet is required. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Derry

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Derry

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. We drain it, disconnect the gas and the vent, carry it out and then install the new tank with fresh connectors, a new drip leg and a properly pitched vent connector. Testing comes last. They are dependable and affordable.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Replacement in Derry, NH

    A gas heater that quits on a weeknight still needs replacing that night or the next morning. FVR Plumbing keeps common gas tanks on the truck for emergencies in Derry, NH. Every emergency swap still gets a leak test, a draft check and a look at the water lines. Same-day is normal.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Derry

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. We connect a new gas flex line with a sediment trap, set the vent connector with a quarter inch per foot of rise and pipe the relief valve to an approved spot. Strapping where required. Your household sets the size.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Derry

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. Plan on a full day. Our licensed plumbers handle all of it to code.
