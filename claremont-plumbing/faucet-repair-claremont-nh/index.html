    Common Faucet Repairs in Claremont, NH

    Compression Faucet Repair in Claremont

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. New washers go in and the seat gets dressed or replaced with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Claremont

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Claremont, NH

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Claremont

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. A new pop-up assembly goes in, matched to your faucet finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Claremont, NH

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Both are quick fixes. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Claremont

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. It shows up on the bill. The worn washer or cartridge gets tracked down and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Claremont, NH

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. The common stems ride on the truck. For filtered water faucets, we replace the cartridge or the check valve as needed.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Claremont, NH

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. Each brand uses its own part number. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.
