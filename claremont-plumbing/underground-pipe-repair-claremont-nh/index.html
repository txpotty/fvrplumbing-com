    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Claremont

    Simple buried pipe repairs usually run $500 to $2,500. Sewer replacements cost more. Extensive trenchless sewer replacements can be $5,000 to $15,000 or more, with location, damage and method driving the total. We quote after inspection.

    A minor leak repair can take a few hours. Bigger jobs run a day or more. A full trenchless sewer replacement could take one to three days, with footage and inspection timing as the main variables. We give you a schedule up front.

    Water lines get PEX or Type K copper. Both are burial rated. Sewer lines get Schedule 40 PVC, and the final choice depends on the existing pipe, the soil and what your local code allows. We match transitions carefully.

    Many utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment. It is worth asking. Most want proof that the leak was repaired promptly, and we give you an invoice and repair documentation that shows the date, location and fix. Policies vary by utility.

    Shallow irrigation leaks are sometimes a fair DIY job. Service lines are different.     For water lines we use PEX or Type K copper. Both are rated for burial. Sewer and drain work uses Schedule 40 PVC, and trenchless jobs use fused HDPE, all of which resist corrosion and handle ground movement better than the old materials they replace. We follow your city's code.

    It depends on your policy and the cause. Check what is excluded. Sudden and accidental damage is sometimes covered, while slow leaks, wear and tear and old pipe failing from age usually are not. Ask your insurer directly. We provide documentation for the claim.
