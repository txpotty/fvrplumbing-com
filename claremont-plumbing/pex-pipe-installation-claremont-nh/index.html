    Why Choose FVR Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Claremont, NH?

    Manufacturer rules for PEX are specific: supports about every 32 inches on horizontal runs, a minimum bend radius, and no long sun exposure. We follow them on every run. Ignoring them causes kinks, sags and strained fittings, and it can cost you the manufacturer warranty that should have backed the pipe.

    • Freeze Tolerance, PEX stretches as ice forms and often survives a freeze that would split copper.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Claremont

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, often a foot and a half to three feet underground.     PEX for Remodels & Additions in Claremont, NH

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines.     Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Claremont

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. A shower valve set two inches too low or a vanity stub that misses the cabinet back means opening the wall again after the tile is up, which is expensive and slow. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. Then we pressure test with the walls open.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Claremont

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Claremont, NH

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.
