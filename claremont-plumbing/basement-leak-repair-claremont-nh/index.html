    How We Fix Basement Leaks in Claremont, NH

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Claremont

    Water lines along a basement ceiling can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Water in much of New Hampshire is soft and often slightly acidic, which can slowly corrode copper pipe and cause pinhole leaks. Bad sections get cut out and replaced, and a run with repeated pinholes gets repiped rather than patched. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Claremont, NH

    Water heaters, softeners and washing machines often sit in basements, and their leaks pool on the floor out of sight. A tank leaking at the seam is done. Leaks at the T&P valve, the drain valve or a supply connector can usually be fixed in one visit, and we add a drain pan or a leak sensor that shuts off the water automatically.

    Water Damage Assessment in Claremont, NH

    Before any repair, we figure out where the water came from and how far it spread. We map the wet zone with a moisture meter and use a thermal camera to see behind finished walls. Guessing costs money. You get photos and readings for your insurance file.

    Groundwater Pressure Relief in Claremont

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. A sub-slab drain line to a sump drops the water table right under the floor. It is not a quick job.

    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Claremont

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Claremont, NH

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale from the inside out. A wet spot at the base of the stack or a crack along the hub is common, and we replace the damaged section with PVC or new cast iron joined with no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. A separated PVC joint usually means the pipe was not fully seated or not supported.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Claremont

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. We cut the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and slope it to a new or existing sump pit before patching the floor with fresh concrete. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Claremont

    Commercial basements hold inventory, records or mechanical rooms that cannot sit in water. We look at floor drains, elevator pits and sump systems as one system, since a single failed check valve or a clogged pit inlet can flood the whole lower level. Duplex pumps help. A high-water alarm warns staff before the floor gets wet.
