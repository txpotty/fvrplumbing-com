    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Claremont, NH

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Claremont

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Claremont, NH

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Claremont, NH

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. We shut everything off, check the meter's leak dial and then follow the leak with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging. Catching it early keeps it small. Most homes have basements, so foundation drainage and sump systems matter for keeping the lower level dry.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Claremont, NH

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Some need replacing soon. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Claremont

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in New Hampshire houses. That list starts with old supply pipe, worn angle stops, aging water heaters, slow drains and sewer lines with roots or sags. Each gets tested, not guessed at. Catching a corroded fitting or a partial sewer blockage now keeps it from becoming a ceiling stain or a backup on a holiday weekend.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Claremont, NH

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. Plan on an hour or so. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Claremont, NH

We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Records matter for inspectors. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.
