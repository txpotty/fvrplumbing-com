    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Claremont

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Claremont, NH

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Repeated leaks on galvanized pipe call for a new line.     Main Water Line Leak Repair in Claremont

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. Confirming it takes about ten minutes.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Claremont

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes mostly rely on copper or PEX supply lines with plastic drains, which hold up better against corrosive water. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. You get a price on every option.
