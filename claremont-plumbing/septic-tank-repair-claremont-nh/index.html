    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Claremont, NH

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Claremont

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. An alarm going off means the water rose past the high-level float. We check the breaker and meter the pump draw before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.

    After-Hours Septic Backup Calls in Claremont

    Late-night backups usually start in the lowest drain in the house, often a basement floor drain or a first-floor shower.     Septic Pipe Repair in Claremont, NH

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age.     Drain Field Repair in Claremont, NH

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload caused by a leaking fixture are the other usual causes. Testing always comes first. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Claremont
