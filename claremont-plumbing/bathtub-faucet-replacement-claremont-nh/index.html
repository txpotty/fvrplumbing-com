    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Claremont

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Claremont, NH

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Claremont, NH

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. Old cross handles can become a modern lever trim, and on a universal valve the wall never opens. Matching the sink faucet is easy. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Claremont, NH

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Claremont

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Claremont, NH

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Claremont

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Claremont, NH

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.
