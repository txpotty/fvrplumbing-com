    Pipe Repair and Repiping in Claremont, NH

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. A single leak gets a clean copper splice or a PEX transition.     Slab Leak Location and Repair in Claremont

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Most homes have basements, so foundation drainage and sump pumps matter for keeping the lower level dry. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. After that, you choose the repair. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute through the attic.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Claremont, NH

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Newer homes mostly rely on copper or PEX supply lines with plastic drains, which hold up better against corrosive water.
