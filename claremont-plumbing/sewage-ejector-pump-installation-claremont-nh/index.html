    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Claremont, NH

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel.     Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Claremont

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Claremont, NH

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. That is the cheapest time, since there is no concrete to cut, and we stub the drains, the vent and the discharge to the plans so the rough-in inspection goes smoothly. Builders like the timing.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Claremont

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. They cost more. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

Stuck floats are common.
