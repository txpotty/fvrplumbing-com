    Commercial Toilet Repair in Claremont

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder.     Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Claremont

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running.     Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Claremont

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Claremont

    Water on the floor can come from four places: the wax ring, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the supply line or sweat running off a cold tank. Each one needs a different fix.     Flush Valve Seat Repair in Claremont

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger.
