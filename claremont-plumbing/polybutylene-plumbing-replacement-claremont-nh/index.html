    PEX Pipe Installation in Claremont, NH

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.

    The Cost of Polybutylene Replacement in Claremont, NH

    Price depends mostly on house size, the number of bathrooms, attic access and whether you pick PEX or copper. PEX is the cheaper option. A planned replacement usually costs less than the drywall, flooring and cabinet repairs after a single burst, and that is before any deductible or mold cleanup gets added on. Every estimate breaks out labor, pipe, permit and drywall.

    Emergency Polybutylene Burst Repair in Claremont

    Poly fittings tend to let go during sharp temperature swings, when the pipe expands and contracts more than usual. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, camps and seasonal homes are especially likely to freeze when the heat is turned down. If a line bursts in your house, close the main shutoff, then call us so we can stop the leak, make a temporary repair and restore water. A full replacement comes next. Waiting for the next burst is the most expensive way to deal with polybutylene.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Claremont, NH

    Some homeowners in Claremont, NH want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.

    Polybutylene to PEX Transitions in Claremont, NH

    Sometimes the yard service line coming into the house is not polybutylene, or it was replaced already. In that case, we connect the new PEX system to the existing service at the main shutoff with an approved transition fitting, which avoids digging up the yard. The service line gets checked. Blue or black poly service pipe should come out as well.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Claremont

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. New pipe goes from the main to every fixture and appliance, each one gets a new quarter-turn stop and the system is tested before any wall is closed.
