    Commercial Plumbing Work We Handle in Portsmouth, NH

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Portsmouth, NH

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless racks get descaled. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Portsmouth

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Portsmouth

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Rocky glacial soil and shallow ledge are common in New Hampshire, which can make digging for sewer and water lines slow and expensive. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. We price both so you can compare.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Portsmouth, NH

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. When pinholes keep moving around the building, repiping the worst zones in Type L copper or PEX usually beats paying for more emergency visits. We map the system first. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Portsmouth, NH

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. Zone-by-zone shutoffs and meter readings narrow it down, then acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and tracer gas pinpoint the spot. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Portsmouth

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.
