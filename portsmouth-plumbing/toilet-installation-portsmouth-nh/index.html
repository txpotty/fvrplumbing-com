    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Portsmouth, NH

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Portsmouth

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. We assemble them on the floor before setting. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Portsmouth, NH

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. A flushometer needs a 1 inch supply and steady pressure. We confirm the building line can deliver the valve's rated flow, often 20 to 25 gallons a minute, before any fixture goes up. Fixture counts follow code.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Portsmouth, NH

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Portsmouth, NH

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Portsmouth, NH

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Portsmouth, NH

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. It adds up quickly. A family of four swapping one old 3.5 gallon toilet can save thousands of gallons a year, and some water providers offer rebates worth checking. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Portsmouth

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.
