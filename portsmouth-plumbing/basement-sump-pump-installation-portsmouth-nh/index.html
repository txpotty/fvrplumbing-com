    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Portsmouth

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Portsmouth homes. They are louder, though.

    Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Portsmouth

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Portsmouth

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. Without house power, a battery backup keeps a second pump going, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We load test the battery.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Portsmouth, NH

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. For those, we step up to a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower unit in a bigger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. We use a stopwatch.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Portsmouth

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in Portsmouth.     Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Portsmouth, NH

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. You hear far less of them than a pedestal, and in Portsmouth, NH we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Portsmouth, NH

A second pump goes into the same pit on its own float switch, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. Your primary pump still handles the everyday load.
