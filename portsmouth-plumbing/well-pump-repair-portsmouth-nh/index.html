    Control Box and Surge Repair in Portsmouth, NH

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. An arrestor costs little.
