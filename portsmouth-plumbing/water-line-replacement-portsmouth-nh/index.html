    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Portsmouth

    Sometimes the open trench wins. A collapsed line, a route with sharp bends, or solid rock can rule out trenchless.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Portsmouth, NH

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and replace the gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Emergency Water Line Replacement in Portsmouth

    Water bubbling up through the lawn or the street side of the sidewalk means a break, not a drip. Close the main shutoff at the house first. If that does not stop it, the break is on your side of the meter but before the valve, and we will close the curb stop with a meter key and plan the replacement from there, usually starting the same or next day.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Portsmouth

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Portsmouth

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years will wear through the pipe. It costs more than PEX.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Portsmouth, NH

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Copper and poly are better pulled than burst. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, and the yard work shrinks to two pits. Rock can stop it.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Portsmouth, NH

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Winterizing unused buildings, draining outdoor faucets and keeping heat on prevent most of the freeze damage plumbers see. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Burst Main Line Response in Portsmouth, NH

    A burst main is a fast-moving problem. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the line is too far gone to bother patching. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.
