    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Portsmouth, NH

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Some need replacing soon. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Portsmouth, NH

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Portsmouth, NH

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. Backflow devices, grease traps, large heaters, flushometers and the main sewer are all part of it. Records matter for inspectors. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Portsmouth

    Water in much of New Hampshire is soft and often slightly acidic, which can slowly corrode copper pipe and cause pinhole leaks. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Portsmouth

    Businesses in Portsmouth use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns. We look at backflow assemblies, water heaters, restroom flush valves, floor drains and trap primers and the building sewer. Downtime costs money. With a ranked report, repairs get scheduled after close instead of forcing a shutdown.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Portsmouth, NH

    Older homes in Portsmouth often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. We mark it on the ground. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.

    Water Heater Inspections in Portsmouth

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.
