    What Our On-Demand Maintenance Covers in Portsmouth, NH

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Portsmouth, NH

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Portsmouth

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. On a Rinnai visit we clean the air intake filter and check the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Portsmouth

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. Spent media gets replaced. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Portsmouth

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Portsmouth

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Portsmouth, NH

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Portsmouth

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Portsmouth

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. On a condensing unit the PVC or polypropylene vent should pitch back toward the heater. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.
