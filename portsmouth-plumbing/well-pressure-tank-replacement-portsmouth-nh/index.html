    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Portsmouth

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. Replacing just the tank can expose other weak spots. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, and give you options for doing the tank alone or the tank plus its fittings. Your call.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Portsmouth, NH

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. A pitted switch can stick on or chatter. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, and we usually recommend replacing a switch older than the tank it served. Cheap part.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Portsmouth

    Big properties need big drawdown. Livestock and irrigation draw a lot. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, so heavy-use systems still keep the pump from short cycling. Floor space matters.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Portsmouth, NH

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. We install them in the common residential sizes. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, then confirming the gauge, switch and drawdown line up with the pump you have. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Portsmouth, NH

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Multi-tank setups are common. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, while planning the work so the building keeps water. Larger tanks need two people.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Portsmouth, NH

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, topping it up to two psi below the cut-in if it reads low. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Portsmouth

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. A leak from the tank itself cannot. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, and a pinhole in the shell means replacement, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Portsmouth

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Air and water share the same space. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.
