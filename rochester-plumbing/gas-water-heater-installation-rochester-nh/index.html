    Propane Water Heater Installation in Rochester

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Different pressure and orifice size mean either a propane model or a factory conversion kit. We call the propane company when needed. We test all fittings.

    Brands We Install in Rochester

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith, Navien and Rinnai make up most of the gas heaters we install in Rochester homes and businesses. We compare warranties, recovery and venting across brands so the choice is easy. Budget matters too. We install all of them.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Rochester, NH

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Rochester

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Most run without electricity. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Installation in Rochester

    Restaurants and laundromats can drain a residential tank in minutes. For businesses we install high-input heaters with bigger gas lines and dedicated vents, sometimes in banks of two or three. Some uses need 140 degrees. Installs can happen after close.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Rochester

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. Each install gets a new gas connector and drip leg, a vent connector pitched upward and a relief line piped where code allows. Strapping where required. Sizing follows household demand.

    Gas Line Upgrades for Water Heaters in Rochester, NH

    Moving up to a bigger or tankless gas heater can outgrow the existing gas line. We size the line from total load and run length so every appliance gets enough gas. Bonding is required on CSST. New lines are pressure tested.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Rochester

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. We inspect the existing vent. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.
