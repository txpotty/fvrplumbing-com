    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in Rochester

    Digging a trench across a mature yard is expensive to restore. Trenchless avoids most of it. We open small access pits at each end, then pull new pipe through the old path or line the old pipe in place, which saves sod, irrigation, pavers and concrete that would otherwise have to be torn out and replaced. Faster, too.

    Pool and Outbuilding Supply Line Repair in Rochester, NH

    Pool auto-fills, guest houses, outdoor kitchens and detached garages often have their own buried water lines. These runs are long. We use tracer wire, electronic locators and acoustic gear to find leaks along the full length, and repairs are made with direct-bury rated pipe and fittings, then pressure tested before we close up. Some lines get rerouted instead.

    Storm Drain and Lateral Repair in Rochester, NH

    Storm laterals carry roof and yard water away from the foundation. When they fail, water pools. We jet the line, camera it for crushed or separated sections and replace the damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water reaches the outlet instead of soaking the ground around your footings. The outlet gets cleared too.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Rochester, NH

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Sewer Line Underground Repair in Rochester, NH

    Roots and settling soil cause most buried sewer failures. Old pipe gives up. We offer open trench repair, pipe bursting and cured-in-place lining, and the camera footage decides which one fits the depth, the damage and what sits on top of the line. All work meets New Hampshire plumbing code.

    Main Water Line Repair in Rochester

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house under constant pressure. Leaks never fix themselves.
