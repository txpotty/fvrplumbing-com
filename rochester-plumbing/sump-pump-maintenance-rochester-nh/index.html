    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Rochester

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. We pump the basin down and scoop out the sediment by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Float Switch Inspection in Rochester, NH

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Rochester, NH

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Rochester, NH

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Rochester

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Rochester, NH

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Rochester, NH

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. They need checking more often than anything in a house. We test the controls, the alarm panel and both pumps in a duplex setup, then log the amp draw on each one so trends show up over time. Downtime costs money.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Rochester

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. Our plumber reads the model number off the label and orders the matching float or seal kit. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne need different checks. We know both.
