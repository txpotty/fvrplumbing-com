    Common Toilet Repairs in Rochester, NH

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Rochester, NH

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. A stiff gasket stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. We replace the whole set of tank seals at once when one is gone.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Rochester

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. If color shows up in the bowl within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. Most of the time a new flapper does it, unless the seat under it is pitted or cracked.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Rochester

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Most commercial toilets use flushometer valves instead of tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. If downtime is a problem, we can come in before you open or after you lock up.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Rochester

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper. The level falls a little until the fill valve opens back up. Other times the refill tube sits too deep in the overflow pipe, which siphons the tank. We clip it above the water line.

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Rochester, NH

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We bring the water up to the line stamped on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A cracked handle arm or a stripped mounting nut takes about five minutes to replace.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Rochester, NH

    Paper towels, wipes, dental floss and toys cause most of the repeat clogs we clear, and none of them break down the way toilet paper does. The closet auger usually hooks them and pulls them out. If the toilet clogs again within a week, the problem is further down the line. Then we look at the branch drain and the main sewer, often with a camera.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Rochester

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall. We tighten or rebuild the carrier and reseal the bowl. That one needs care. Leave a loose wall-hung bowl alone long enough and the china can crack at the mounting holes.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Rochester

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.
