    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Rochester

    A well pump that clicks on and off every few seconds usually points to a failed pressure tank. A ruptured bladder lets the tank waterlog, the air cushion disappears, and the pump starts with every tap, wearing the motor out years early. A new tank fixes it. We also check the switch.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Rochester, NH

    Switching to tankless during a replacement is common in Rochester, NH, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually needs to be upsized, and the vent changes from metal B-vent to stainless or PVC. We price both up front.

    Water Heater Replacement in Rochester

    Water heaters in Rochester that leak from the tank, rumble on every heat cycle or run out of hot water halfway through a shower are usually cheaper to replace than to keep repairing. Tank leaks are not repairable. We match the fuel and size, update the venting and add an expansion tank or drain pan if code calls for one. Hot water is back by evening.

    Well Pump & Pressure Tank Replacement in Rochester

    Rural homes around Rochester that run on a well have no water at all when the pump quits. We pull the old submersible pump, check the wire and drop pipe for damage, set the new pump at the right depth and test flow and pressure, often replacing the pressure tank and switch at the same visit. Most jobs finish same day.

    Pipe Replacement and Repiping in Rochester

    Corroded, leaking or galvanized pipe in Rochester can stain water and cause costly damage. Failing sections or the whole house get new PEX or copper, routed to keep openings small and pressure tested long enough to catch any slow drip. Walls get patched.

    Sewer Line Replacement in Rochester

    Sewer lines in Rochester fail from roots, cracks, collapses and bellies where the pipe sank and now holds waste. Rocky glacial soil and shallow ledge are common in New Hampshire, which can make digging for sewer and water lines slow and expensive. We run a camera first.     Water Softener Replacement in Rochester, NH

    Softeners wear out as the resin breaks down and the control valve stops cycling properly. Water in much of New Hampshire is soft and often slightly acidic, which can slowly corrode copper pipe and cause pinhole leaks. Once scale returns, dishes spot or salt stops dropping, we test hardness, size a new unit and plumb it with a bypass so the house keeps water during service. Salt use often drops too.
