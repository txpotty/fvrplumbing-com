    Pilot Light and Ignition Service in Rochester

    No hot water and a dark pilot window usually point to the ignition side of the heater. The tech pulls the pilot assembly and checks the thermocouple tip, the orifice and the igniter wire. Common pilot kits ride on most of our trucks.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Rochester

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. We read the error code first. Every suspect part then gets tested with a meter.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Rochester

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. We clean the screen and the chamber. A tripped thermal switch gets replaced.
