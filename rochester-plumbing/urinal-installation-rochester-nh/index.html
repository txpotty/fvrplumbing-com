    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Rochester

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Rochester

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Rochester

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Models like the Washbrook and the Bardon come in versions from 0.125 to 1.0 gallons per flush, and each one needs a flush valve matched to that rating or the bowl will either splash or not rinse clean. Matching is simple with the spec sheet. We order both together.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Rochester

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.
