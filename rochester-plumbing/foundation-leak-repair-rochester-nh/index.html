    Leak Detection Tools We Use in Rochester, NH

    Infrared cameras, acoustic listening gear and tracer gas cover most leaks we chase in Rochester, NH. Gas helps when the leak is too quiet to hear. A hydrogen and nitrogen blend goes into the empty pipe, and a sniffer picks it up where it rises through the slab, even when the leak is silent.
