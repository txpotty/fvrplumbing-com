    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Rochester, NH

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines.     Hydronic Heating with PEX in Rochester

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, though not the same pipe you drink from. Heating work calls for oxygen-barrier PEX, coated so oxygen cannot seep through the pipe wall and corrode the boiler, the circulator and the iron parts. Loops have length limits. Run them too long and the last stretch never warms up.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Rochester, NH

    Think of a manifold as a breaker panel for water. A central header feeds each fixture on its own line with its own valve, which lets you shut off one toilet and leave the kitchen running. You buy more pipe. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Rochester

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. A shower valve set two inches too low or a vanity stub that misses the cabinet back means opening the wall again after the tile is up, which is expensive and slow. We confirm heights with your contractor first. The test happens before drywall goes up.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Rochester, NH

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.
