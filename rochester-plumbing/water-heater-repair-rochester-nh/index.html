    Pilot Repair in Rochester

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Less often it is a dirty flame arrestor screen choking off combustion air. The thermocouple gets a millivolt test. If it reads fine, the gas control valve is next.

    Tankless Water Heater Troubleshooting in Rochester

    Error codes on a tankless unit narrow the problem down, but they do not always name the part that actually failed inside the cabinet. Similar codes can come from a flow sensor or an igniter. Water in much of New Hampshire is soft and often slightly acidic, which can slowly corrode copper pipe and cause pinhole leaks. Descaling comes first, since scale causes more tankless trouble than worn parts do. Then sensors.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Rochester, NH

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. Hard water can eat through one in a few years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems. Rotten-egg odor often clears up.

    Gas Burner and Venting Repair in Rochester, NH

    Combustion is the part of a gas heater that matters most for safety. Yellow flame tips or soot at the draft hood mean the burner is not getting enough air to burn cleanly. The burner gets cleaned, and draft gets tested at the hood. Backdrafting is dangerous.
