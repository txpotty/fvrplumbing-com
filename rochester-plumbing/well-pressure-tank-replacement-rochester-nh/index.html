    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Rochester, NH

    Same-Day Tank Swaps in Rochester

    We keep common tank sizes on our trucks. That means a call in the morning can often end with a new tank installed by afternoon, including new tank tee, gauge and relief valve, without waiting on a parts order. Bigger tanks may take a day to order.

    Emergency Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Rochester

    A tank that bursts or a pump that runs nonstop needs attention fast. Turn the pump breaker off right away. A pump that keeps running against a failed system can overheat and burn out, and we aim to swap the tank before the pump suffers. Water back tonight.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Rochester

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. First step, kill the breaker to the pump. Then we drain the system, unthread the old tank from the tank tee, set the pre-charge on the new one, and plumb it back in with fresh thread sealant and new fittings. Usually a morning.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Rochester

    Big properties need big drawdown. Stock tanks, irrigation and barns add up. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, so heavy-use systems still keep the pump from short cycling. Floor space matters.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Rochester

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. Replacing just the tank can expose other weak spots. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, and give you options for doing the tank alone or the tank plus its fittings. Your call.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Rochester, NH

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Switch and tank are a team. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, and a cheap new gauge beats guessing. Small parts, big role.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Rochester

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Rochester

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.
