    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Rochester, NH

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Rochester, NH

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Rochester

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. A same-size drop-in swap usually takes a morning, and a deeper bowl may only need a new trap. New counters add a day. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Rochester

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. We install stainless sinks with commercial faucets and basket strainers, mount them to carry a full bowl of water plus a stack of dishes, and add a hand sink where your inspector wants one. Downtime gets planned around your hours. Night installs are possible.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Rochester, NH

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Rochester

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Rochester, NH

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. For an accessible bathroom we set the rim no higher than 34 inches, leave knee clearance underneath, and wrap or shield the hot water and drain pipes so a seated user cannot touch them. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Rochester

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Old basket strainers fuse to the bowl, the disposal has to come off its mounting ring, and the dishwasher drain hose needs to be rerouted to the new disposal or tailpiece with a high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Rochester, NH

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.
