    Why Choose FVR Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Rochester, NH?

    A grease trap works by slowing the water down long enough for fat to float and food to sink. Past about 25 percent full, the separation stops working. The grease then slips past the outlet into the lateral, cools into a hard white crust, and a cable machine only bores a narrow hole through it.

    • Complete Pump-Outs, We pull grease, water and the settled solids every time.
    • Code Compliance, Most cities, Rochester included, set a pumping rule for food service kitchens, commonly based on 25 percent of depth, and inspectors check the log.
    • System Longevity, Less grease in the line means fewer jetting calls and a longer life for the drain pipe.

    Grease Line Jetting in Rochester

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. The buildup forms rings that are thickest along the bottom. Snaking just bores a small path that closes up in weeks, but a rotating jetter nozzle strips grease off the full wall of the pipe. A camera confirms it afterward.

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Rochester

    Indoor traps usually sit under the three-compartment sink or in a recessed pit near the dish station. Room to work is tight.     Compliance Documentation for FOG Programs in Rochester, NH

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Rochester, NH

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. A jetter takes it off the pipe wall. A camera pass afterward shows whether the line is really clean.
