    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Rochester

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Rochester

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. A clean pipe holds a liner best.
