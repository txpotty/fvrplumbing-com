    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rochester, NH

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Rochester, NH homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Rochester

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment.     Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Rochester, NH

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Rochester, NH

    The housing stock in New Hampshire includes many old farmhouses and village homes, often with cast iron drains and galvanized supply lines.
