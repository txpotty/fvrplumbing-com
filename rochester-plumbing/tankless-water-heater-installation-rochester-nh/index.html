    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Rochester, NH

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. Venting changes too. Both are on the estimate.

    Cost-Effective Tankless Conversions in Rochester, NH

    A tankless conversion in Rochester, NH costs more than a tank swap, mostly because of the gas line upgrade, the new venting and the unit price itself. The savings come back over time. With no standby loss and a lifespan that can reach 20 years, many homeowners see the difference on the gas bill within the first few winters. We give you real numbers.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Rochester

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Rochester, NH

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Testing well water around Rochester is the right first step before choosing a neutralizer or filter. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.
