    Slab Leak Reroutes in Rochester

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. It suits a first-time leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Rochester

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. Lead-free solder only. Because some water chemistries eat copper, we look at your leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Rochester, NH

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Rochester, NH

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Our crew cuts neat rectangles, covers nearby surfaces and patches each opening after the pressure test. We match the texture. We match knockdown, orange peel or smooth walls, so the patches are ready to prime and paint.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Rochester

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. We stop the active leak first, make temporary repairs that get water back on and then plan the full repipe so you are not rushed into it. That gives you time. Your policy may pay for water damage even if not for the new pipe, so we take photos of everything we find.

    PEX Repiping in Rochester, NH

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. Flow stays strong at the last shower.
