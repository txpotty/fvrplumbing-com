    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Rochester

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. Old-style constant-wattage heat tape can overheat and is a known fire risk, so we install self-regulating cable listed for the pipe material and replace worn or brittle heat tape we find.

    Preventive Pipe Insulation in Rochester

    Foam pipe insulation is cheap and stops most freezes in attics and crawlspaces. It has to fit. We size the sleeve to the pipe, tape every seam and miter the corners so there are no gaps at elbows and tees, since a single bare fitting is often exactly where a line freezes first.

    Post-Thaw Pipe Inspection & Repair in Rochester, NH

    A pipe that froze and thawed without leaking may still be damaged. We pressure test it to find out. A gauge on the isolated line shows a hairline split within minutes.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Rochester

    Drain lines can freeze too, mostly in crawlspaces and outside walls. You will see the backup first. We thaw drains with a steam or hot-water jetter fed through the cleanout, then check the slope, because a drain that holds water in a sag is the kind that freezes again next time.

    Water Heater Line Thawing and Repair in Rochester

    Water heaters in garages or attics have exposed supply lines that can freeze. Tankless heaters on outside walls freeze easily. Freeze protection on tankless units needs power, so after an outage we check the heat exchanger and connectors.

    Preventive Pipe Winterization in Rochester

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Rochester, NH
