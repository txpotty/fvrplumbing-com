    How We Replace Polybutylene in Rochester, NH

    PEX Pipe Installation in Rochester, NH

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Rochester, NH

    Some homeowners in Rochester, NH want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, and join it with lead-free solder or press fittings. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Rochester, NH

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. These buried lines can split under the yard, raising the water bill and soaking the soil. Utilities get marked first. Then we trench the route, install new PEX or copper service pipe and connect it to a new main shutoff at the house.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Rochester

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Testing well water around Rochester is the right first step before choosing a treatment system or filter. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Emergency Polybutylene Burst Repair in Rochester

    Poly fittings tend to let go during sharp temperature swings, when the pipe expands and contracts more than usual. Winterizing unused buildings, draining outdoor faucets and keeping heat on avoid most of the freeze damage plumbers see. If a line bursts in your house, close the main shutoff, then call us so we can stop the leak, make a temporary repair and restore water. Then we plan the full fix. Living with poly until the next failure costs the most in the end.
