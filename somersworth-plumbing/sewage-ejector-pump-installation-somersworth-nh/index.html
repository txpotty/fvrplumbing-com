    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Somersworth, NH

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Somersworth, NH

    Liberty builds simplex and duplex packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Somersworth

    Ejector pumps usually last 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Watch for constant running, a hum or grind, repeated alarms and odor at the pit, since any of them means trouble is close. Old switches fail first.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Somersworth

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Somersworth, NH

    Upgrading an old ejector can often keep the existing pit. With a sound basin, a new pump, fresh check valve, shutoff and alarm give you most of a new system for far less. We tell you if the basin is done.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Somersworth

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Frost heave and shifting rocky soil can damage older sewer laterals and shallow water lines. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. Floating basins crack floors.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Somersworth

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. Grinders run pricier. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Somersworth, NH

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. With no concrete to cut, it costs the least then, and we stub drains, vent and discharge to the plans for a clean rough-in inspection. It keeps the pour on schedule.
