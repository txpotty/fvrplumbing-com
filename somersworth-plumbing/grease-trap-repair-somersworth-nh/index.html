    Residential Grease Trap Repair in Somersworth, NH

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, but some have one, usually a small plastic unit under the kitchen sink on a house with a septic system or a home bakery.     Flow Control Device Repair in Somersworth

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate.     Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Somersworth

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Somersworth, NH

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Somersworth

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Somersworth, NH

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined.     Damaged Baffle Repair in Somersworth

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. A cracked or loose baffle turns the trap into a pass-through. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.
