    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Somersworth, NH

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Somersworth, NH

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. Finished basements are the usual reason.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Somersworth, NH

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Somersworth

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Somersworth, NH

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Backups add cost. We list each option on a written quote.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Somersworth

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Somersworth

    A pump swap in an existing pit usually runs $600 to $1,500. A new pit with slab cutting and digging often runs $1,500 to $4,000, depending on how deep we go and what we hit. Battery backups add $500 to $1,500.

    Plenty of cities require a plumbing permit for new sump pump installs or major replacements. FVR Plumbing takes care of permitting in Somersworth, NH so the work passes inspection.

    Our trucks carry Zoeller, Liberty, Wayne and Flotec pumps. We choose based on lift, inflow and budget.

    A straight replacement often needs no permit. We let you know if your job needs one, usually only for new pits or wiring.

    The pump has its manufacturer warranty, and FVR Plumbing warranties the installation labor. Exact terms depend on the pump model, and they are written into your quote and service paperwork.

    For some homeowners, swapping a pump in a working pit is doable. Anything involving a new pit, a new discharge route or new wiring is a job for a professional.

    Grinding, rattling or a pump that runs with no water in the pit are all warning signs. Visible rust, a float that sticks or a pump that cycles every minute during dry weather usually means the end is near. If the basement flooded anyway, it is time.

    Most standard installs take three to six hours. If we have to cut the slab, dig a new pit and patch the concrete, plan on most of a day, with the patch curing overnight. Swaps go faster.
