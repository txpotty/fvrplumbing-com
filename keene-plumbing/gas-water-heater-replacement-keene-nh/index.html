    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Keene, NH House

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Keene

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. We drain the old tank, cap the gas, and set the new heater on a stand or pan. New flex connectors every time. We soap-test the gas joints and check the draft hood with the burner lit.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Keene, NH

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Keene, NH

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    Commercial Gas Tank Replacement in Keene

    Laundromats and multi-unit buildings work commercial tanks so hard that replacement comes on a predictable schedule rather than as a surprise. We plan the swap around your slow hours. Some jobs need added vent or gas work.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Keene, NH

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Keene

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of New Hampshire plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.
