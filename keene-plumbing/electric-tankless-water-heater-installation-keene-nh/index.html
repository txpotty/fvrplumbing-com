    Guest suites, detached studios and additions often sit far from the main water heater. Long hot lines waste water and heat, so a nearby electric tankless is often the better choice. We size it for the space. No vent needed.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless Installation in Keene

    A guest bath or a far kitchen sink can take a minute to get hot water from the main heater.     Residential Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Keene

    Residential electric tankless installs start with counting fixtures that run at the same time. A household running two showers at once in cold weather usually needs a 27 kW unit on three 40 amp breakers. One bath needs less. We install Rheem, EcoSmart and Stiebel Eltron.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Keene, NH

    Upgrading from a 50 gallon electric tank to tankless is mostly an electrical project. Where the tank used one 30 amp circuit, a whole-home tankless often needs two or three 40 amp circuits. The water side is simple. We remove and dispose of the old tank.

    Whole-Home Electric Tankless Systems in Keene, NH

    Whole-home coverage in Keene, NH means one unit big enough for your busiest hour. Sizing uses peak fixtures and the coldest water, and sometimes two smaller units beat one big one. Every tap stays hot. We plan it before quoting.

    EcoSmart units are popular for their digital temperature control and low price. FVR Plumbing sets EcoSmart units in homes across Keene, NH. We check the panel first, because the larger EcoSmart models need three 40 amp circuits. Then we set the temperature.

    Rheem Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Keene

    Rheem's RTEX line covers most of the whole-home electric tankless units we install.
