    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Keene, NH

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Keene, NH

    Rheem and AO Smith make tanks in nearly every size, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We help you compare.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Keene

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. You see both numbers.
