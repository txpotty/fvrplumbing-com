    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. Backsplash comes last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Keene, NH

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Keene

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. A cast iron farmhouse bowl can top a hundred pounds empty, so the base cabinet often gets reinforced before it goes in. The cabinet gets checked before install day. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Keene, NH

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Keene

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Keene

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. A wall bracket lagged into blocking carries the weight, and if the wall has none, we open it and add a board. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Keene

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Keene

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.
