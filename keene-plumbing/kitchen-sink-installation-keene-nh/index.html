    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Keene, NH: The Details

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Keene

    Drop-in sinks are common in New Hampshire homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim holds the sink up. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Next we connect and test the faucet and drain.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Keene

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Keene get their commercial sinks installed by FVR Plumbing. Hand sinks need tempered or hot and cold water. We install three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks with the indirect drains, floor sinks and grease trap connections the health inspector will look for.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Keene, NH

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Keene

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Composite Sink Installation in Keene

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Composite makers usually call for silicone instead of plumber's putty under the strainer, since some putties stain the material, and we follow that sheet.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Keene

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Keene, NH

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Keene, NH

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out cannot be patched for long. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, so a same-day swap is often possible, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. Old stops come out too.
