    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Keene, NH

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Keene, NH

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Water in much of New Hampshire is soft and often slightly acidic, which can gradually wear down copper pipe and cause pinhole leaks. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. The difference shows on camera.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Keene

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    Root Removal Jetting in Keene

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Roots come back through the same cracks, so the camera shows where a repair or liner would stop them.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Keene, NH

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Keene

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Keene, NH

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Keene, NH

    Retail and office buildings have long horizontal drain runs that collect buildup. Flat runs collect more debris. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.
