    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral commonly runs $4,000 to $12,000. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000.     Commercial Trenchless Sewer Repair in Keene, NH

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom.     Root Intrusion Repair in Keene, NH

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Keene, NH

    Depth changes the math. A line two feet down in open lawn is often cheaper to dig than to line, but a lateral eight feet deep that runs under the driveway and a porch costs far more to excavate than to burst or line from two pits. We quote both ways. Then you pick.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Keene, NH

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Frost heave and shifting rocky soil can damage older sewer laterals and shallow water lines. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.
