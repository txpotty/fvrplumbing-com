    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. Ports go along the crack, the face gets sealed, and resin goes in from the bottom up until it fills the full depth. Epoxy adds strength. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Keene

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. A battery backup is worth adding where power drops in storms.
