    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Exeter

    Prices in Exeter, NH usually land between $800 and $2,500. A pedestal unit runs cheaper than a cast iron submersible, and the biggest swing comes from whether we have to excavate a new pit and whether you want a battery backup added to the same basin. Estimates are itemized.

    Labor on our sump pump installs is warrantied, typically for one year. The pump carries its own manufacturer warranty, usually one to five years depending on the brand and model, and we write the exact coverage on your invoice so you know which part to call about if something fails. Keep the receipt.

    Liberty Pumps, Zoeller and Wayne are our regulars. All three use cast iron housings and reliable switches, and all three have solid parts support through local supply houses, which matters more than a fancy feature list when something breaks. We match the model to your pit.

    We strongly recommend professional installation. You are dealing with wiring, pipe and concrete in one job, and a bad seal on the lid, an undersized pit or a discharge that drains toward the house can leave you worse off than having no pump at all. Mistakes show up in storms.

    Most standard installs finish within 4 to 8 hours. A new pit or a long discharge run adds a few hours, and if we need a new circuit from the panel, an electrician may need to come out the same day, which can push the job into the evening. The timeline comes with the quote.

    A 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower pump handles most residential basements. The real answer depends on inflow and lift, so we time the refill with a stopwatch and measure from the pit floor to the discharge exit before picking a model. Bigger is not always better.

    Every two to three months, and before and after the wet season. Pour a five gallon bucket of water into the pit, then watch the float lift, the pump start, the water drop, and the pump shut off cleanly without humming or rattling. That is the whole test.
