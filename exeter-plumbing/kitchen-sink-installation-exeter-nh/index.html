    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Exeter

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Exeter, NH

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Exeter, NH

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.
