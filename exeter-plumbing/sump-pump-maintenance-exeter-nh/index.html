    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Exeter

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. Burned-out motors get a new pump installed before we leave, so the pit is protected again that day.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Exeter

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Exeter, NH

    A new sump pump, installed, often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower, pit work and whether you add a battery backup. Routine maintenance costs a small fraction of that. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care. You get a straight answer on whether your pump has life left or should be replaced.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Exeter

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. Our plumber pulls the unit out so we can see the cord and the shaft seal. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Exeter, NH

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Exeter

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Exeter

    Once a year is the minimum. Each yearly visit covers the motor, the impeller, the float and the discharge line. Then we fill the pit and time how long the pump takes to empty it, because a slow drawdown is the earliest sign of a worn impeller or a partly blocked line.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Exeter, NH

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. Winterizing unused buildings, draining outdoor faucets and keeping heat on prevent most of the freeze damage plumbers see.
