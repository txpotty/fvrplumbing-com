    How We Find Slab Leaks in Concord, NH

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Concord

    Plastic pipe muffles leak noise. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, even through grout lines and small cracks. It catches leaks the microphone cannot in Concord homes.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Concord

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, listening for the spot where the sound peaks and then checking it again from a different angle, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Concord, NH

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Urgent Slab Leak Calls in Concord, NH

    A slab leak that is soaking carpet or pushing water up through tile cannot wait until next week. Shut off the main. FVR Plumbing sends a tech to Concord, NH homes quickly, isolating the leak so you have water elsewhere while we plan the repair.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Concord

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. Tile and hardwood floors make this the favorite.

    Slab Leaks in Commercial Buildings in Concord

    Commercial slabs are thicker and carry larger pipe, which means more water lost and fainter sounds at the surface. Our approach changes for those. Our techs combine zone isolation, line tracing and tracer gas, and schedule testing around your operating hours in Concord.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Concord

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Concord, NH home.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Concord, NH

    A second slab leak is often a sign of more. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, ending the cycle of floor openings and surprise water bills. Most repipes wrap up in two to four days.
