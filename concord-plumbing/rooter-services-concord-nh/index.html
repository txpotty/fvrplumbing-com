    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Concord

    A single clogged sink or tub in Concord, NH usually runs $200 to $350. Main sewer lines with roots or jetting tend to land between $400 and $1,000. Access matters too.     Chemicals sometimes open a small hole in a hair clog, but they will not touch roots or a grease plug. Repeated use can weaken old PVC joints and pit cast iron. They are dangerous to handle. A cable takes the clog out instead of melting a narrow path through it, which keeps the drain open longer.

    Drain snaking, also called augering, uses a flexible cable and cutting head to break up or pull out the clog. Hair, wipes and roots respond well to it. Hydro jetting washes the entire inside of the pipe with high-pressure water, removing grease, scale and sludge the cable leaves on the walls. Repeat clogs usually need jetting.

    Rooting is safe with a trained tech and the correct cable and blade. Collapsed or badly cracked pipe can be hurt by anything run through it. The tech's touch matters. If the cable tells us the pipe is damaged, we stop and send in a camera before going further.

    Plan on 1 to 3 hours for a single clogged drain. A root-choked main or a long jetting job may take 3 to 6 hours. You get a time estimate first. Once the tech sees the cleanout and asks which fixtures are slow, they can give you a closer estimate of how long it will run.

    Snaking runs a spinning cable with a cutting head through the clog. A jetter uses water at thousands of psi to scour the inside of the pipe from wall to wall. Jetting cleans more. Snaking handles one-time clogs, while jetting is for grease lines and scaled mains that keep coming back.

    We run RIDGID, Spartan and Gorlitz augers, plus sewer cameras and jetters from the same tier of commercial makers. These machines take daily abuse and keep working. Brand matters less than setup.
