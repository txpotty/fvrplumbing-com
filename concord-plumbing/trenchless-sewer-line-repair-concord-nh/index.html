    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Concord

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Concord, NH
