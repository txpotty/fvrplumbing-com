    How We Install Showers in Concord, NH

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Concord, NH

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. On a slab, we cut concrete. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Concord

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Concord

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Pair a rain head with a handheld and body sprays and a 40-gallon water heater may run cold halfway through. We check the heater. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Concord

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Pressure varies by house. A gauge reading comes first.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Concord

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. Replacing an old pan, converting a tub to a shower or swapping in a new valve and trim can make a big difference without tearing the bathroom down to the studs. You see each option's price. They tend to wrap up within a week.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Concord

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Then we test. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Concord, NH

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. A cracked pan or a leaking valve means pulling the shower, checking the framing and getting a new unit in as soon as it is safe. Dry framing first. Then the new shower goes in.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Concord, NH

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Handles and the head come later. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.
