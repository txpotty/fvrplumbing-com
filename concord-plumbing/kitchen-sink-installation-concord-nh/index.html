    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first.     An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Strainers and traps go in after.
