    Septic Code and Permit Work in Concord, NH

    New tanks and new fields need a permit from the local health authority in most of the country, along with any change to the system's layout.     Drain Field Repair in Concord, NH

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload caused by a leaking fixture are the other usual causes. We test before recommending anything big. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Concord, NH

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Concord, NH

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. Our techs rebuild linear aerators and swap rotary ones, and we clear diffusers that have plugged with sludge. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Concord

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. We measure sludge and scum on every maintenance visit, and we pump when the solids reach about a third of the tank depth. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Concord

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Every lid we set is screwed down or locked so a child cannot lift it.

    Commercial Septic Repair in Concord, NH
