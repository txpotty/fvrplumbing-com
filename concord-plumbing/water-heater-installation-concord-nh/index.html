    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Concord

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Carbon monoxide is the risk. All work follows local code.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Concord

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. Our crews install to spec. That protects your coverage.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Concord

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Water heater replacements, new fixtures and sewer or water line work usually require a permit where the town enforces a code. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Concord, NH

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. You get more spots to put it.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Concord

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Concord, NH

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Every manufacturer sets its own clearance, venting and relief valve rules, and following them is what keeps the warranty good. We know those manuals well. We help you compare models on warranty length and recovery rate.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Concord, NH

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Concord

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank typically runs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installs often run $3,000 to $6,500, depending on how much gas line and venting work the house needs.

    Our crews install Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai, all brands with solid parts support and decent tank warranties. Our pick depends on your budget.

    Start with correct sizing and a thermostat set at 120 degrees. Insulating the first few feet of hot pipe and flushing the tank once a year both help, and a tankless model cuts standby loss.

    Permits are common for water heater work. Water heater replacements, new fixtures and sewer or water line work usually require a permit where the town enforces a code. FVR Plumbing handles the permit and the inspection in Concord.

    Age, repair cost and efficiency decide it. If the heater is over ten years old or the repair costs more than half of a new one, replacing it usually makes more sense. A leaking tank is always a replacement.

    An expansion tank absorbs the extra pressure created as water heats. Homes with a closed plumbing system are often required by code to have one, since it protects the heater and every pipe in the house.

    Doing it yourself means handling gas, 240-volt power and pressurized water at once. Mistakes can cause gas leaks, CO exposure or electrical fires, and a DIY install can void the warranty. We do not recommend it.
