    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Concord, NH

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Concord

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run.     Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Repair in Concord

    Expect $120 to $250 for a simple flapper or handle job and $200 to $500 for a base reset or a clog that goes past the trap into the drain line. A broken flange adds a bit more. We quote a flat price once we see the toilet, before any work begins.

    Swapping tank parts or resetting a toilet on a new wax seal generally does not need a permit. Moving the drain or adding a new toilet usually does, and the rules come from local code, so they vary from one town to the next even within New Hampshire. We look it up before work begins.

    Watch for phantom refills at night, a hiss from the tank, a toilet that shifts when you sit, and stains or soft flooring around the base. Repeated clogs belong on that list too. Catching these early keeps a flapper job from turning into a subfloor repair.

    Most of it comes from a worn flapper or a scaled flush valve seat, and the rest from a fill valve set so high that water spills into the overflow tube. A short chain can also hold the flapper open. With the lid off, you can usually spot it within a minute.

    Flapper and fill valve jobs take twenty to forty-five minutes. Pulling the toilet and resetting it on a new wax ring takes about an hour, and a flange repair adds another thirty to sixty minutes depending on what is under the floor. Deep clogs take longer.

    A loose chain or a float set too high is a fair DIY fix, and so is swapping a flapper if you buy the right size for your flush valve. Bring the old flapper with you to match it. If the toilet still runs after that, the seat or the fill valve is usually the problem, and that is a good time to call FVR Plumbing.
