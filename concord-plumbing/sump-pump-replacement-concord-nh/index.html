    Which Sump Pump Suits Your Concord, NH Home

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Concord

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Figure on an hour or two for a typical swap. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Concord, NH

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We match the old pump or step it up. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Concord

    Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise groundwater quickly in New Hampshire, sending water into basements that stay dry most of the year. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Concord, NH

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. Worn units get replaced, and we reset the float rod stops so the cycle is right. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Concord, NH

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. We move those calls to the front of the line. Our tech shows up with a pump and valve on the truck, and in most cases water is leaving the pit within an hour. After that, we look at why the old one quit.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Concord, NH

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. Discharge goes away from the foundation. Test them each spring.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Concord

    Horsepower matters less than people think. A third-horse pump moves plenty of water for most homes, as long as the vertical lift to the discharge is under about 15 feet. Before we choose, we read the pump curve at your real discharge height. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Concord

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. Then a new check valve goes on. Skip that part and the pump short cycles.
