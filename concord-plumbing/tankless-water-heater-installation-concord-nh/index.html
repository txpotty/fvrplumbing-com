    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Concord, NH

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Water in much of New Hampshire is soft and often slightly acidic, which can gradually wear down copper pipe and cause pinhole leaks. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. Service valves make it easy.

    Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in Concord, NH

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem in Concord, NH homes all year long. Rinnai has a wide model range and good parts support, Navien builds in recirculation on many models, Noritz makes compact condensing units that fit tight spaces, and Rheem pairs well with their tank lineup. Warranty terms vary. We walk you through them.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Concord, NH

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing models use PVC vent and a condensate drain.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Concord

    Gas tankless units in Concord are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. Gas is the catch. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We test the new gas piping before first fire.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Concord

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. We weigh both. A far bath may need recirc.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Concord, NH

    Replacing an old tankless unit is usually simpler than a first-time conversion. The gas line is already sized and the vent path already exists, so in many homes it is a matter of matching the connections, checking the vent for damage and hanging the new unit. Vent sizes can change by brand. We confirm fit first.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Concord

    Propane homes in Concord can run tankless units too, using a model built or converted for propane. Since propane runs at different pressure from natural gas, the regulator, line sizing and gas valve must all match, and the supplier may need to swap the tank regulator. Tank size matters too.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Concord

    Commercial hot water loads in Concord run longer and harder than a house. A restaurant can run a dish machine, hand sinks and a mop sink together, and the rack must hold temperature through the rush. We rack units with a common manifold. Service one, keep the others going.
