    Anode Rod Replacement in Lebanon

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. We swap worn ones. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Lebanon

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Bad venting is dangerous. We test the gas connection too.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Lebanon

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Testing well water around Lebanon is the right first step before choosing a neutralizer or filter. The tank gets drained until it runs clear, then refilled, and the anode, relief valve and connections get checked. Plan on an hour. Less sediment means more years.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Lebanon

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Lebanon, NH

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. We flush it with descaler. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Electric Element Service in Lebanon, NH

    Two elements, two thermostats. A burned-out lower element is the usual cause of running short on hot water. Power gets shut off first. Good parts stay in.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Lebanon, NH

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. We swap bad ones. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.
