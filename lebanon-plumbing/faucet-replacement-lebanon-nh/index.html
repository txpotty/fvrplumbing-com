    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Lebanon

    Pull-down spray heads take a beating. The hose wears, the spray button sticks, and after a few years the docking magnet or weight stops pulling the head back into place. We swap the whole faucet. We clip the new hose weight at the right height and connect fresh braided supplies to both stops.

    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Lebanon

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. The pull-down hose wears where it bends over the spout neck, and once it splits, water runs down the inside of the faucet and into the cabinet without anyone seeing it. Sometimes a new hose is enough. If the body is also worn, the whole faucet gets replaced along with the supplies.
