    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Lebanon, NH

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Lebanon, NH

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Lebanon

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.
