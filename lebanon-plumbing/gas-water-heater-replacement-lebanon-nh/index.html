    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Lebanon, NH Home

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Lebanon

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most New Hampshire homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We check clearance before we order. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Lebanon, NH

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. That is all part of the job. Downtime stays short.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Lebanon, NH

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. More warranty years generally buy a heavier anode rod. We help you pick.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Lebanon, NH

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. Bad vent sections and draft hoods get replaced. We check draft with a match or mirror at the hood.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Lebanon

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. Sizing and gas hookup are part of the job. We confirm the vent meets their specs.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Lebanon, NH

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Lebanon

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Lebanon, NH

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.
