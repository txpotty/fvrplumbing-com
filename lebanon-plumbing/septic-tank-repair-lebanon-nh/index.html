    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Lebanon

    Repair Versus Replace in Lebanon, NH

    Most septic systems that fail can be repaired for a fraction of the cost of a new one. You see the repair and the replacement priced next to each other, and we tell you which one we would pick for our own house. A new field is sometimes the right call. Often it is not.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Lebanon, NH

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Lebanon, NH

    Some tanks have no riser, so we look at the baffles through the small inspection port with a push camera. That saves digging up the whole lid. If the outlet tee is cracked or missing, we dig at that end only, then cut in a new PVC tee and an effluent filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Lebanon

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. We jet each lateral from the distribution box or a cleanout at the far end, and what washes back shows how plugged it was. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Lebanon

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. After pumping, we inspect the walls and the joints with a strong light, and a dye test in the drains shows where water comes in or goes out. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    Leach Field Restoration in Lebanon, NH

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A box that has settled or cracked can push all the effluent into a single trench. A new or leveled box brings a lot of fields back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.
