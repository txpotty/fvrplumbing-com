    What Goes Into a Water Heater Replacement in Lebanon, NH

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Lebanon, NH

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Lebanon, NH homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Lebanon

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A bigger tank or a higher first-hour rating handles more back-to-back showers, and sometimes a 50-gallon high-recovery unit matches a 75 in the same footprint. Bigger is not always needed.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Lebanon, NH

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option in Lebanon, NH. Tankless and hybrid units cost more up front but can lower operating costs, and tankless often lasts 15 to 20 years versus about 8 to 12 for a tank. We price all three.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Lebanon, NH

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We help you compare.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Lebanon

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Lebanon, NH

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Lebanon

    The housing stock in New Hampshire includes many old farmhouses and village homes, often with cast iron drains and galvanized supply lines. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. We bring the help.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Lebanon

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. When a tank starts leaking at the base, making loud popping sounds or giving rusty hot water, we replace it like-for-like or resize it, and the whole job usually takes two to four hours from shutoff to first hot shower. Hot water by evening.
