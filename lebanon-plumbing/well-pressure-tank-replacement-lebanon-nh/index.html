    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Lebanon, NH

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Lebanon

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Lebanon, NH

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. It works with the tank, not apart from it. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Lebanon

    Big properties need big drawdown. Livestock and irrigation draw a lot. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, so heavy-use systems still keep the pump from short cycling. Floor space matters.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Lebanon, NH

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, topping it up to two psi below the cut-in if it reads low. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Lebanon

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Lebanon, NH

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. Pressure stays steady even with two showers running. These systems can run on a smaller tank, but they cost more up front and need a compatible pump and drive. We explain the trade-off.
