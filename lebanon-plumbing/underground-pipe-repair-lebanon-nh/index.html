    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Lebanon

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, so we replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. Pressure comes back right away.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Lebanon, NH

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. Collapsed sections are fine. Lining cannot do that.

    Emergency Underground Pipe Services in Lebanon

    Buried Sewer Lateral Repair in Lebanon, NH

    Buried laterals crack from roots, settling and age. Video locates the break. We excavate at the mark, cut out the damaged length and replace it with Schedule 40 PVC, using shielded transition couplings that match the old and new pipe. Fall gets checked before we close it.
