    Disposal Repair in Lebanon, NH: The Details

    Humming Disposal Repair in Lebanon

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Lebanon, NH

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Lebanon

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. We start at the red reset button on the bottom, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. Cabinets are damp places. Corroded wire nuts and loose cord connections are common in that damp space, and we replace them rather than just tightening them back down.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Lebanon, NH

    Paying half the price of a new disposal for a repair seldom pays off, and we will say so. Many fixes are cheap. A reset, a cleared jam, a new switch or a resealed flange usually costs far less than a new unit and can keep a younger disposal going for years.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Lebanon, NH

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Lebanon

    Jams usually trace back to one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Check the trap arm if it jams again.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Lebanon

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Lebanon, NH

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. Common kits ride on our trucks. That keeps most repairs to one visit.
