    Hands-On Sump Pump Maintenance for Lebanon, NH Homes

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Lebanon

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. We test duplex systems and high-water alarms on a set schedule. Logs go to the property manager.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Lebanon

    Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise groundwater quickly in New Hampshire, sending water into basements that stay dry most of the year. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Lebanon, NH

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Lebanon

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. Sooner or later the pump sucks it into the screen and locks up the impeller. Our plumber pumps the basin down, rinses the screen and inspects the liner for cracks that let dirt seep in through the walls. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Emergency Sump Pump Repair in Lebanon

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. We send a plumber with floats, check valves and spare pumps on the truck. A jammed impeller usually clears in a few minutes. A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Lebanon, NH

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Float Switch Checks in Lebanon, NH

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Lebanon, NH

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. Frozen pipes are a regular winter risk in New Hampshire, especially during long stretches of subzero nights and strong wind.
