    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Lebanon

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Most homes have basements, so foundation drainage and sump systems matter for keeping the lower level dry. Before anyone cuts the floor, we check the gutters, downspout extensions and the grade along that wall, because fixing those outside often stops seepage for a fraction of the cost. We always try the cheap fixes first.

    Sump Pump Repair and Installation in Lebanon, NH

    Plenty of basements rely on a sump pit to handle runoff and groundwater. A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are smart protection for any finished basement. Cheap pumps tend to last about five to seven years, and a pump that hums or never shuts off is near the end. We replace pumps the same day.

    Emergency Basement Leak Repair in Lebanon, NH

    A pipe that lets go in a basement can put an inch of water on the floor in under an hour. Shut the main valve first. Then call us, and a plumber will come out to stop the leak, repair the pipe, and get water back on so you can start drying the space. We can also refer a water mitigation company.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Relief in Lebanon

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. You may see water coming up at the floor cracks or around the floor drain during a long wet week. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.

    Basement Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Lebanon, NH

    A simple fitting or supply line repair usually costs $200 to $600. Cast iron stack work tends to land between $500 and $2,000. Sump installs run $1,200 to $2,500, and perimeter drain systems cost several thousand more because of the concrete and digging. Prices vary with access.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Lebanon, NH

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Expect a few days of work. We bring in a waterproofing partner for the membrane and handle the footing drain and its tie-in to a sump or daylight outlet ourselves.

    Urgent Basement Leak Service in Lebanon, NH

    Basement leaks rarely wait for business hours. A water heater tank can split in the middle of the night. The on-call truck carries couplings, valves and a wet vac, so the first visit usually stops the leak and restores water to the rest of the house.

    New Sump Pump Installation in Lebanon

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.
