    Pressure Tank Replacement Options in Laconia, NH

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Laconia, NH

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. It is one of the most common tanks we install. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, limiting internal corrosion, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Laconia, NH

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. The diaphragm splits air from water. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, including fast cycling and water at the Schrader valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Laconia, NH

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Some run four or more on a manifold. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, while planning the work so the building keeps water. Larger tanks need two people.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Laconia

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, plus they are easier to move into cramped utility rooms. Same plumbing as steel.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Laconia, NH

    A bladder tank holds water inside a rubber balloon surrounded by compressed air. The air pushes water out when you open a tap. Bladders crack with age, especially if the tank ran with low pre-charge, which waterlogs the tank. Replacement is the fix.

    Emergency Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Laconia

    A tank that bursts or a pump that runs nonstop needs attention fast. Turn the pump breaker off right away. A pump that keeps running against a failed system can overheat and burn out, and we aim to swap the tank before the pump suffers. Water back tonight.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Laconia, NH

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. A pitted switch can stick on or chatter. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Laconia

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. First step, kill the breaker to the pump. Then we drain the system, unthread the old tank from the tank tee, set the pre-charge on the new one, and plumb it back in with fresh thread sealant and new fittings. Usually a morning.
