    PEX Pipe Installation in Laconia, NH

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. On a whole-house repipe that means fewer joints hidden behind drywall and fewer places for a slow leak to start rotting a stud. It handles a freeze better than rigid pipe. Winterizing unused buildings, draining outdoor faucets and keeping heat on prevent most of the freeze damage plumbers see. That stretch is not a license to skip insulation, so exterior-wall and crawl-space runs still get wrapped.

    Water Heater Replacement in Laconia

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment. We install 40 and 50 gallon tanks, plus 75 gallon units for big households, from Rheem and Bradford White, with new flex connectors, a relief line piped to code and a vent check on every job. An expansion tank check is part of every job. One visit covers most installs.

    Water Service Line Installation in Laconia, NH

    New water service lines run from the meter to the house, and the old one usually comes out in the same trench. Frost heave and shifting rocky soil can crack older sewer laterals and shallow water lines. We bring the digging equipment the ground calls for. We add a new main shutoff at the entry point. Depth and bedding protect the pipe. Pressure and flow get tested before the trench is closed.

    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Laconia

    Testing well water around Laconia is the right first step before choosing a neutralizer or filter. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations. A unit sized too small regenerates constantly and wastes salt. The drain line gets a proper air gap. Programming follows, set to your hardness, and we run one test cycle.

    Sump Pump Installation in Laconia, NH

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. The pit goes to proper depth, the primary pump is sized for the inflow, and a battery backup is added on request. Where the water exits matters most. The discharge gets carried well away from the foundation so the same water is not pumped twice.

    Backflow Preventer Installation in Laconia

    Irrigation systems and fire sprinkler lines are the usual reasons a home needs a backflow preventer. It keeps fertilizer-laced lawn water from being pulled back into the drinking supply when a main break or hydrant flush drops pressure. Type matters. We pick the assembly for the hazard level and local requirement and test it on install day.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Laconia, NH

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Laconia, NH

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. A PRV usually means you need one. Fixtures below the sewer line get an ejector system with a sealed basin, a check valve and a vent.
