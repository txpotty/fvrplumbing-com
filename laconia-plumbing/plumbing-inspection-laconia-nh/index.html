    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Laconia

    Most home plumbing inspections run $150 to $350, depending on the size and age of the house. Sewer video is an add-on. A sewer camera adds to the total, and you hear the full price before we arrive, so the invoice matches the quote.

    Most residential inspections in Laconia take one to two hours. Older or bigger homes take longer. House size, plumbing age and whether a leak or sewer problem needs extra tracing all affect how long the visit lasts.

    High water bills, repeat clogs, slow drains and discolored water are common signs. Noisy pipes are another. Knocking pipes, a rumbling heater or mold near fixtures usually mean something underneath needs a professional look.

    For an older home, it usually pays for itself. Old pipe hides problems. Homes with galvanized supply pipe, clay or cast iron sewers or original water heaters often have problems that are invisible from the outside, and an inspection lets you plan repairs instead of reacting to a failure.

    Our technicians inspect all major water heater brands, including Rheem, AO Smith and Bradford White. Tankless is included. Rinnai and Noritz tankless units and most household fixture brands are routine, so your equipment brand will not be a problem.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Gurgling drains count too. Damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell under a sink or a warm patch of tile can all point to a hidden leak that an inspection will find before it does more damage.

    Most homes do well with an inspection every one to two years. Before buying is a good time too. If you are purchasing a property, live in an older house or have had recent leaks or backups, a yearly check gives you a better chance of catching the next problem early.
