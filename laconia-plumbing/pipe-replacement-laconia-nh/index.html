    PEX Pipe Replacement in Laconia

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, camps and seasonal homes are especially likely to freeze when the heat is turned down. It often survives freezing where copper would crack. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Laconia

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.
