    Quality cast iron pumps usually carry three to five years of factory coverage. We back our installation labor separately, and we walk you through the exact terms for the model you pick before we install it.

    A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any finished basement. Outages and storms arrive together, so we recommend a battery backup in most basements. It switches on by itself when the main pump loses power, and it also catches the water if the primary switch sticks or the pump simply cannot keep up with a heavy inflow. It is cheap insurance.

    Mostly cast iron pumps from Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Parts for all four are easy to find at supply houses, which matters when a switch fails on a Sunday and you want the pump running again without waiting a week for an order to ship. We stock the common models.
