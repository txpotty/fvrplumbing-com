    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Laconia, NH

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is thinner and mostly used inside the house. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against stone contact and soil chemistry. Same fittings, different pipe.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Laconia

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Laconia, NH

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. Plenty of copper mains have lasted 60 years. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Laconia, NH

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each has a place. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, meeting NSF 61.

    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Laconia

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. It does not need the old pipe as a guide. We use it when the new line has to take a different route, say around a new addition or under a driveway poured after the house was built, leaving just two small pits in the yard. Heavy rock is the exception.
