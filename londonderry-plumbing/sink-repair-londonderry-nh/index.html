    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Londonderry, NH

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Londonderry

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Londonderry

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Londonderry, NH

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Londonderry

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.
