    Why Polybutylene Fails in Londonderry

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Testing well water around Londonderry is the right first step before choosing a neutralizer or filter. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Londonderry, NH

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost.
