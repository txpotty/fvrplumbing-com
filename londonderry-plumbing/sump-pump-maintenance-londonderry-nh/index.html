    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Londonderry

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. Our plumber pulls the unit out so we can see the cord and the shaft seal. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Float Switch Checks in Londonderry, NH

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Next we adjust the on and off levels so each run is longer and the starts are fewer.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Londonderry

    A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are smart protection for any finished basement. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Londonderry

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. In elevator pits, the pump usually needs an oil-sensing control so it will not discharge hydraulic fluid. Duplex systems and high-water alarms get tested on a fixed schedule. The property manager gets a copy of every log.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Londonderry, NH

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Londonderry

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. That debris gets pulled into the intake and jams the impeller. We pump the basin dry, rinse the screen and check the plastic liner for cracks, since a cracked liner lets soil wash in from the sides. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Londonderry, NH

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Londonderry

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. We still check the casting for cracks and the float for free travel. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.
