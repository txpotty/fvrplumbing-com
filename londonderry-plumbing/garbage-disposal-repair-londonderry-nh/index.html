    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Londonderry, NH

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Londonderry

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Londonderry

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring, and we also test air switches and batch-feed lids that use a magnet to start the motor. Most are simple fixes.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Londonderry, NH

    Jams and minor leaks usually cost far less than a new unit. If the disposal is past eight to ten years old, leaks from the body or has a failing motor, replacement is usually the better value, and we give you both prices when it is close. The choice is yours.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Londonderry

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Londonderry

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. We start at the red reset button on the bottom, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink. In that damp cabinet, wire nuts corrode and cord connections loosen, so we replace them instead of retightening.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Londonderry

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Londonderry

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually include a control box and a solenoid valve for water. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. Controls get checked first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Londonderry, NH

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. Side leaks are usually the discharge gasket or the dishwasher hose clamp.
