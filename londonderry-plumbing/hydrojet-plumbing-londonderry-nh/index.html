    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Londonderry, NH

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Londonderry

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. The camera always goes in first. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Londonderry

    Properties with chronic drain issues benefit from scheduled jetting. Restaurants jet every three to six months. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Londonderry, NH

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Londonderry

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers need drains that keep up with heavy use every day. A backup closes rooms or stores. We set up jetting plans for each building's needs, schedule work during slow hours, and keep the camera footage on file so property managers can show tenants or owners the condition of the lines.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Londonderry

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Londonderry, NH

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.
