    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Londonderry, NH

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet.     Propane Gas Leak Repair in Londonderry, NH

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising.
