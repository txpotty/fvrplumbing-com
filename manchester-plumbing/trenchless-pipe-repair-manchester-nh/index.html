    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Manchester

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Manchester

    Older Manchester homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Pipe Bursting in Manchester, NH

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Manchester, NH

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. We burst or bore. Our crew pulls new HDPE or PEX through the old path with a bursting head, or drills a fresh path with a directional boring tool, so lawns, driveways and walkways stay untouched. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Manchester, NH

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Then add restoration. Open trenching under a drive or garden adds concrete, plant and sprinkler replacement to the bill, which trenchless mostly avoids. We price both so you can compare.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Manchester

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. Short damage gets a short liner. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, a sectional liner costs less than a full-length one and goes in quickly, but we confirm the rest of the line is sound by camera before recommending it.

    Sectional Point Repair in Manchester

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Manchester sewer lines.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Manchester

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Manchester yard.
