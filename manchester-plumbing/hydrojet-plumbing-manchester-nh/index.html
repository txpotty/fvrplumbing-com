    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Manchester

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Manchester

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. The camera always goes in first. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Manchester

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Hold off on running water. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Manchester, NH

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Manchester, NH

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Water in much of New Hampshire is soft and often slightly acidic, which can gradually wear down copper pipe and cause pinhole leaks. Scouring nozzles remove the scale, and badly scaled cast iron may get a descaling pass too. The difference shows on camera.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Manchester, NH

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Manchester, NH

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Manchester

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers rely on drains that handle heavy daily use. A backup closes rooms or stores. We schedule around slow hours and keep video records for property managers.
