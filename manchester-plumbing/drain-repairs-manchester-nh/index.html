    Drain Repair Options for Manchester, NH Homes

    Bathroom Drain Repair in Manchester

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Scale speeds it up. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, plus tub and shower drain leaks that stain the room underneath. Most of these are one-hour jobs.

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Manchester, NH

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. The repair gets a flow test.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Manchester, NH

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. Fine soil plugs it over time. Rocky glacial soil and shallow ledge are common in New Hampshire, which can make digging for sewer and water lines slow and expensive. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, and we locate the bad section on camera before replacing it with wrapped perforated pipe in washed stone. The outlet gets checked as well.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Manchester

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Repeat clogs are a warning. We clear the line with a drum auger or a jetter, then run a camera if the clog returns within a few weeks or sits more than a few feet past the trap, which shows whether you have buildup, a sag or a broken fitting. The footage tells us what to fix.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Manchester, NH

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, so we cut out the failing section and replace it with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Manchester

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Remodels cause a lot of these. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, while fixtures that never had a vent can sometimes get an air admittance valve if code allows. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in Manchester, NH

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. They clog the most. We clear the branch, replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and reset traps that were installed too low or too high for the trap arm to drain properly. The disposal and dishwasher hookups get a look too.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Manchester, NH

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Repairs meet local plumbing code.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Manchester

    A simple clog clearing starts around $150. Bigger repairs climb fast. Complex main line repairs can run past $3,000, depending on the drain involved, the extent of the damage and the access. We price it after we look.

    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Bigger changes might. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Manchester may require a plumbing permit, depending on how much pipe is replaced and how the city defines the work. The permit is on us if needed.

    Tightening a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is fair game. Hand snakes are fine too. Anything inside a wall, under a floor or involving cast iron, or a repeat leak at the same joint, should go to a plumber. Skip the caustic cleaners.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair fixes the pipe. A crack, a sag, a broken fitting or a missing vent will keep causing clogs no matter how often the line gets cleaned, so a drain that clogs repeatedly usually needs repair rather than more snaking. We check on video.

    Fixing the cause instead of the clog. Pitch is the big one. After we correct a sag, replace a rough fitting or add a missing vent, the line stays clear with normal use and a little care about grease. Older lines may still need yearly cleaning.
