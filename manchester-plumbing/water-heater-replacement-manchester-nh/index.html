    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Manchester

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. A bigger tank or a higher first-hour rating handles more back-to-back showers, and sometimes a 50-gallon high-recovery unit matches a 75 in the same footprint. Recovery rate counts too.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Manchester, NH

    Ruud and Rheem are built by the same maker and share many parts. Our technicians install both, and since the parts overlap, a Ruud thermostat or element is easy to source when a repair makes more sense than a full replacement on a newer tank. Repair stays an option.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Manchester

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. When a tank starts leaking at the base, making loud popping sounds or giving rusty hot water, we replace it like-for-like or resize it, and the whole job usually takes two to four hours from shutoff to first hot shower. You shower tonight.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Manchester, NH

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Manchester, NH homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Manchester

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Manchester, NH

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Manchester, NH

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option in Manchester, NH. Tankless and hybrid heat pump models cost more to buy and install but can cut operating costs over the life of the unit, and tankless units often last 15 to 20 years compared to roughly 8 to 12 for a typical tank. Ask for each option.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Manchester, NH

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We lay out the options.
