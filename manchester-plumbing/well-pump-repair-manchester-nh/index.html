    Well Pump Problems We Fix in Manchester, NH

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Manchester

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. A properly sized tank can add years to pump life.

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Manchester

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Manchester, NH

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. Our technicians test circuits with the power locked out. All wiring and splices meet New Hampshire electrical code before the pump restarts.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Manchester

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow. Testing starts at the surface. If it has to come up, a pump hoist lifts the pipe safely, and we replace the burned motor, worn impellers or a bad check valve before setting it back.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Manchester, NH

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. When they fault, the drive shows a code that guides diagnosis. The drive, sensor and wiring get tested, and the failed part gets repaired or replaced before we verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Manchester, NH

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. FVR Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Manchester, NH

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any finished basement. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. Arrestors are cheap.

    Well Pump Maintenance and Repair Cost in Manchester, NH

    Maintenance costs far less than an emergency pull. A typical well pump repair in Manchester runs $400 to $1,500, while a yearly checkup costs a fraction of that. FVR Plumbing prices both up front. The checkup covers tank precharge, switch contacts, amp draw and the well cap seal, which catches most problems before the pump fails in the middle of a hot week.
