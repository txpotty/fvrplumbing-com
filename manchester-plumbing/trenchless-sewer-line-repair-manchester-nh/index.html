    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Manchester, NH

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years.     Pipe Bursting in Manchester, NH

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. The new HDPE pipe is butt-fused into one length first. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. It goes quickly.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Manchester, NH

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.
