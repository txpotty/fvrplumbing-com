    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Manchester

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Manchester

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. Venting still applies.

    Emergency Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Manchester

    An ejector failure often starts with the high water alarm, followed by slow drains and a smell near the pit. Shut off water to the basement fixtures, keep everyone from flushing below grade, and call us, since most emergency pump replacements are done in a few hours once the plumber is on site. We carry common pumps.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Manchester

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Liberty sells packaged systems with basin, lid, pump and check valve already matched, which means less field fitting and a listed assembly for inspection. A full-port valve gets added.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Manchester, NH

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours. A new system with a basin, slab excavation, venting through the roof, electrical work and an alarm costs more, but it is the only way to add a bathroom below the sewer line in most homes without a macerating unit. Ask for both numbers.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Manchester

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. They cost more. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.
