    Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Manchester

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and either an air gap at the sink or a high loop under the counter, depending on local code. Fridges need a cold line. We run braided stainless or copper to the fridge with a quarter-turn shutoff, never the thin plastic tubing that splits behind the fridge.

    Disposal Installation and Upgrades in Manchester

    Most families do fine with a 1/2 horsepower unit, though heavy cooks and big families get more from 3/4 or 1 HP. Each install gets a new mounting assembly, a new discharge tube and a punched dishwasher knockout if you have a dishwasher. Every unit runs with water before we leave. A dry towel goes around the flange and discharge tube after.

    Grease Trap Cleaning in Manchester

    A grease trap that smells sour is normally overdue for a pump-out. We remove the lid, scrape the baffles, pull the floating grease and settled solids, and check the gasket so the lid seals again and odor stays inside. We log every visit. Inspectors usually want to see that log before anything else.

    InSinkErator Installation in Manchester, NH

    Many homeowners ask to step up from an older InSinkErator to an Evolution because the newer units are quieter and grind finer. Often the mount stays. When the old Quick Lock mount is sound, we can reuse it, which saves time and leaves the sink flange alone, and we register the warranty for you.

    New Disposal Installation in Manchester

    Adding a disposal where there never was one means a switched outlet or circuit in the cabinet, and that part is electrical work. We handle the plumbing and can line up the electrician. The trap often has to drop a couple of inches because the disposal outlet sits lower than the sink tailpiece did, and the wall stub has to allow it.

    InSinkErator Models We Service in Manchester

    The Badger series is the entry line, with a single grind stage and a 1/3 to 3/4 horsepower motor. Evolution models add grind stages and sound insulation, which you notice in an open floor plan, and some carry longer in-home warranties than the basic line does. We stock the common sizes. Older InSinkErator units with a good motor can usually be repaired.

    Replacing an Old Kitchen Sink in Manchester

    Pulling an old cast iron sink takes two people and a putty knife, because they weigh a lot and are usually sealed hard to the counter. The counter cutout gets measured first. Your new sink must fit the existing opening or the countertop needs cutting, so we confirm the size before you order. The drain gets rebuilt to suit the new bowl depth.

    Kitchen Drain Clearing in Manchester, NH

They sit in the trap and soften PVC glue joints over time.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Kitchen Plumbing in Manchester

    Most clog clearing falls between $150 and $300. A garbage disposal replacement including the unit typically runs $350 to $600, depending on horsepower and whether the mount can be reused. Repiping a branch line can go past $800, and we price it after seeing the pipe.

    Watch for slow drains, a sour smell from the sink, gurgling when the dishwasher drains, or a disposal that hums but will not grind. Look in the cabinet as well. A warped or stained cabinet base means a slow leak has been going for a while.

    Swapping a faucet or clearing a clog usually does not need one. Relocating a sink, adding a drain or altering the waste and vent lines usually requires one, though each jurisdiction sets its own rules. We check Manchester rules ahead of the job.

    Straight swaps usually do not. Moving a drain or installing a grease trap often does, and we take care of the permit when needed.

    Most restaurants need it every one to three months, depending on menu, volume and local rules. Fryers shorten the interval. A house with a small grease trap is usually fine with one inspection and cleaning a year, unless the family does a lot of frying.

    Most commercial kitchens clean every one to three months, though a busy fryer kitchen may need it every few weeks. Most houses do not have one. A common rule is to pump it before grease and solids take up about 25 percent of the trap, and some sewer districts set the interval for you.

    Rice, pasta, coffee grounds and grease collect in kitchen drains. Scale from hard water makes the buildup grab faster. A professional cleaning plus a few better habits at the disposal usually stops it for good, unless the line has a sag or poor slope.

    Humming without grinding, water under the sink, a bad smell that cleaning does not fix, frequent clogs or total silence when you flip the switch. Check the reset button first.
