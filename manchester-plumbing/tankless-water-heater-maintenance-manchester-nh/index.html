    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Manchester

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Calcium and magnesium drop out on the hottest tube walls first and narrow the passages. A 45 to 60 minute circulation with descaling solution dissolves it.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Manchester, NH

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Manchester

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Manchester, NH

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. We take a flow and temperature reading on each heater. Then we compare them.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Manchester

    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.
