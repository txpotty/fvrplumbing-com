    PEX Repiping in Manchester

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings.     Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Manchester

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. One moves groundwater, the other moves sewage, and they are not interchangeable. We size before we sell. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Manchester, NH

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. CSST must be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Sump Pump Installation in Manchester, NH

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. We dig the pit to the right depth, set a primary pump sized to the inflow and add a battery backup if you want one. Where the water exits matters most. We run the discharge line far enough from the foundation that the water does not cycle straight back into the pit.
