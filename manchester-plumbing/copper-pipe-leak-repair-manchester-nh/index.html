    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Manchester, NH

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. Repeated leaks change the math. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system, so repiping often beats another round of spot fixes on total cost. We price both.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Manchester

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. You see a drip or a stain. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, which is why we use thermal imaging to find them before we open anything. We replace the pitted length with Type L. Nearby pipe gets checked.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Manchester

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Minerals jam them. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, so we replace failing valves and fittings with quarter-turn ball valves that shut off fully and are easy to turn. We test each one.

    Copper Slab Leak Repair in Manchester

    Many slab homes have copper water lines running under the concrete. Listen for running water. Both get a price.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Manchester

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with Type L copper or a press fitting. More pinholes often follow.

    Copper Leak Behind Walls in Manchester, NH

    Small pinholes can suddenly widen and spray water into a wall or ceiling. Locate your shutoff. We help you shut off the water over the phone and send a plumber to locate and repair the leak, and if the damage is extensive, we make a safe temporary fix to restore water while we plan the full repair. You will know what needs drying.

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Manchester

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. There is less metal to lose. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, replacing the damaged length with Type L copper so the repair has a thicker wall than the pipe it replaced. We check the rest of the run.
