    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Nashua

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Nashua, NH

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.
