    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Nashua

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Nashua, NH

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Every manufacturer sets its own clearance, venting and relief valve rules, and following them is what keeps the warranty good. We know those manuals well. You can compare warranty length and recovery rate with us before you buy.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Nashua

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Clearances, maximum vent lengths and relief valve rules differ by brand, and warranty claims get denied when an installer skipped them. Our crews install to spec. That protects your coverage.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Nashua

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Gas or electric both work. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Nashua, NH

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Nashua

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Nashua

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. New Hampshire enforces a statewide plumbing code based on the International Plumbing Code, and plumbers are licensed by the state. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We set the air charge to match your water pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Nashua, NH

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Leak checks follow. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.
