    What Our Nashua, NH Drain Techs Clean

    Main Line Drain Cleaning in Nashua

    Toilets bubbling while the washer drains? The main line is the likely spot. Every fixture shares that one pipe, so the lowest drain in the house is where water comes up, and the usual causes are roots, a crushed section of old pipe or a heap of wipes caught on a bad joint, which we find with a camera and clear with a sectional cable or a jetter.

    Outdoor & Storm Drain Cleaning in Nashua

    Yard drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and roots. Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise groundwater quickly in New Hampshire, sending water into basements that stay dry most of the year. Standing water near the foundation is the warning sign. We jet or cable the line to the outlet and pull the debris out of the catch basin, so the next storm runs off instead of soaking into the soil next to the house.

    Restaurant and Shop Drains in Nashua, NH

    Commercial kitchens fight fats, oils and grease every day. The grease interceptor helps, but lines still coat. A regular jetting schedule, set by how much frying your kitchen does, keeps the line open, and we leave a service report you can show the health inspector or the landlord.

    Bathroom Sink, Tub and Shower Drains in Nashua, NH

    Toothpaste and soap scum glue hair into a stringy plug. Pop-up stoppers catch it first. We pull the stopper, clean the trap and send a small cable down the branch line toward the stack, and if the stopper keeps catching hair we can adjust it or swap it for one with a simpler design. Usually under an hour.

    Camera Inspection After Drain Cleaning in Nashua

    A camera shows what the cable felt. Self-leveling heads keep the picture upright.
