    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Nashua

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Most run without electricity. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Nashua

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. Plan on a full day. We do all of it to code.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Nashua, NH

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. A 120-volt outlet is required. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Nashua

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.
