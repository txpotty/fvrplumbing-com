    Whole-Home Repiping in Nashua

    Repiping sounds bigger than it is. Most homes stay livable during the job. We work room by room, restore water most evenings, and keep dust down with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, then patch every opening before we leave. Clean finish.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Nashua

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Nashua, NH

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Close the main shutoff right away. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and our crews aim to stop it fast. Drying comes next.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Nashua

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Downtime costs money. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, keeping restrooms and kitchens running while we work on other sections. Night work available.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Nashua, NH

    We often leave old galvanized pipe in the wall. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Nashua, NH

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    PEX Repiping in Nashua

    PEX repipes go faster than copper. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, while manifold setups give you separate shutoffs. Popular choice.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Nashua, NH

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.
