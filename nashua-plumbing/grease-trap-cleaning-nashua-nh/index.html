    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Nashua, NH

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. Our jetters run around 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle, which scours the whole inside of the pipe and pushes the loosened grease downstream. Afterward a camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Nashua

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. We pump each compartment on its own. On big tanks we break the grease cap with a pressure wand first, since a cap that has set up hard will plug the suction hose and leave half the grease behind. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.

    Grease Trap Inspection and Repair in Nashua

    An empty trap is the only time anyone can see what is wrong with it. Cracked baffles and missing outlet tees are the usual finds. Without an outlet tee, the grease cap flows right into the sewer, and older concrete interceptors lose that tee more often than you would think. We can often fix small problems on the spot.

    Emergency Grease Trap Services in Nashua, NH

    Gray water coming up through the floor sinks in the middle of service is the classic sign the trap has hit capacity. We send a truck the same day when we can.     Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Nashua, NH

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. That crust survives a normal pump-out. Jetting the empty tank and its inlet line strips it back to bare walls and gets back the capacity that crust was taking. Plan on about an extra hour.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Nashua, NH

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. In a busy kitchen, that means cleaning every week or two. We take the lid off, remove grease and solids, scrape the baffles, check the flow control fitting on the inlet, and replace the lid gasket if it is flattened or torn. A bad gasket is why most of these smell.

    Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Nashua

    A trap that stinks between pump-outs usually has a flattened lid gasket or a vent problem. We check both on every visit. A gasket costs little to replace, while a missing or broken vent can let sewer gas into the kitchen and also slow the trap's drainage, so that one is worth tracking down. Odors are often fixed the same day.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Nashua, NH

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. Every compartment gets opened and pumped to the bottom. While the tank is empty, we look at the inlet and outlet tees, the baffle wall, the risers and the lid seals, because a cracked riser lets groundwater and dirt in. Lids go back on tight.
