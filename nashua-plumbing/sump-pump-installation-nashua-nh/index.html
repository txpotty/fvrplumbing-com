    Storm-Ready Sump Pump Installation in Nashua, NH

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Nashua, NH

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Access is often tight. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Nashua, NH

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Nashua, NH

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. People with carpet downstairs buy these. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Nashua

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Nashua, NH

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Nashua

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Nashua, NH

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Replacement switches are easy to get. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Nashua

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. Lids close flat over them. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.
