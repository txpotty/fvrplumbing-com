    What Our Tankless Maintenance Covers in Nashua, NH

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Nashua

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Nashua, NH

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. Gas pressure gets checked with every burner lit, not at idle. We pull and clean the inlet screens and air filters. Downtime is the real cost. We can book it before you open or after you close.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Nashua

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Nashua, NH

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. Future flushes then take minutes to set up.

    Noritz Descaling in Nashua, NH

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Nashua

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Nashua, NH

    No hot water before work is a rough start. Most no-heat calls come down to a failed igniter or an exchanger so scaled that the high-limit switch keeps tripping. The truck carries igniters and descaling gear, so we can usually fix it that day.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Nashua

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. On these units we pull the air intake filter, wash it and check the flame rod for carbon buildup. Recirculating Rinnai models also have a pump setting worth checking, since a wrong schedule wastes gas every hour the pump runs for no reason.
