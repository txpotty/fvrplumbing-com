    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Dover, NH

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Dover

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In an older house that usually means rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with tired fittings, and a new faucet cartridge will not touch it. Repiping does fix it. Every line from the main valve to each angle stop gets replaced, and the system is tested before anything gets closed up.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Dover, NH

    Apartment buildings, small offices and churches in Dover, NH are good candidates for PEX as much as houses can. With a manifold and shutoff for each unit, a leak in one apartment only takes that apartment offline, not the whole riser. Rated walls need firestopping. Where PEX passes through a fire-rated assembly, we use listed firestop sleeves or sealant so the rating holds.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Dover, NH

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Dover

    The main from the meter to the house feeds every fixture you own. A leaking old service line shows up as a soggy patch in the yard, a higher water bill and weak pressure everywhere. New PEX goes in its own trench. At the foundation we install a new full-port ball valve as the main shutoff.

    New Construction PEX Plumbing in Dover, NH

    On a new build, FVR Plumbing runs PEX supply lines while the framing is still open in Dover, NH. We drill the plates, set the manifold and stub out every fixture to the heights on the plans, then hold a pressure test for the rough-in inspection. Nail plates go on wherever pipe crosses a stud. Builders like the pace.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Dover, NH

    Price on a repipe depends on a short list: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Drywall patching may be separate. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Dover, NH

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines. While the studs are exposed we can run PEX back to a manifold or a sound trunk, set fresh stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a fridge line. Additions get their own sizing. A new bath at the far end of the house may need a three-quarter inch feed to keep pressure up.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Dover

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, but a different kind you drink from. Heating loops need oxygen-barrier PEX, which has a coating that keeps oxygen from migrating through the wall and rusting the boiler, the pump and any iron in the system. Loops have length limits. Run them too long and the last stretch never warms up.
