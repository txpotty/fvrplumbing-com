    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Dover, NH House

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Dover, NH

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Dover

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Dover, NH

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Commercial code is tighter. Sizing comes from peak demand and recovery rate.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Dover, NH

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. More warranty years generally buy a heavier anode rod. We help you pick.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Dover

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Dover

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. We disconnect the old unit and reconnect the new one to the existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. We confirm a closet install has louvers or ducted air before we light the burner.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Dover

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. We drain the old tank, cap the gas, and set the new heater on a stand or pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. Then we leak test with soap solution and check draft at the hood.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Dover, NH

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.
