    What We Jet in Dover, NH, Line by Line

    Hydro Jetting for Hard Water Scale in Dover

    Water in much of New Hampshire is soft and often slightly acidic, which can slowly corrode copper pipe and cause pinhole leaks. Scale traps debris. Clogs then form faster. Jetting breaks down and flushes out hard mineral deposits, restoring the full diameter of the pipe, and it works much better than chemical descalers that can also damage older pipe.

    Kitchen Line Grease Jetting in Dover, NH

    Grease clings and hardens. Flat runs are the worst. Jetting melts and scrubs that buildup away instead of poking a hole through it, using smaller hose and nozzles sized for 1-1/2 and 2 inch kitchen lines, so your sink and dishwasher drain properly for much longer.

    Older Home Drain Jetting in Dover

    Older homes collect decades of buildup. Grease and soap scum lead. We use nozzles that work through the bends in your pipes and clear years of buildup, at pressures that are safe for most residential plumbing and with nothing but water going down the drain.

    Root Cutting With Hydro Jetting in Dover

    Specialty nozzles slice fine roots. Root hairs slip past a saw. Roots from yard trees and shrubs often enter at clay pipe joints or cracks, and jetting clears them out and gives a clean view of each entry point on camera afterward, which helps you weigh lining against a spot repair.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Dover

    Cast iron grows rust tubercles. Scale adds to them. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and restore flow, while we keep the pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots, which is also the standard prep before pipe lining.

    Emergency Hydro Jetting Services in Dover, NH

    A full sewer backup needs fast help. Our jetting trucks run after hours. The emergency crew arrives with jetting equipment and a camera, clears the blockage and restores flow, so water stops coming up through the lowest drain and the damage stops spreading.

    Emergency Hydro Jetting in Dover, NH

    Backed-up lines cannot wait. We respond fast. An emergency tech brings a jetter and a camera, clears the line and limits the damage as quickly as possible.

    Hydro Jetting Cost Compared to Snaking in Dover, NH

    Jetting costs more on the first visit. The results hold up better. If you are paying for snaking several times a year, jetting often costs less over time because it removes the coating that causes the repeat clogs in the first place, though a simple soft clog may only need a cable.
