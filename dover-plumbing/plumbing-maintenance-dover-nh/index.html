    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Dover

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Dover

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. Brine, injector, resin and regeneration all get checked, and we test the water at a tap. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Older resin may need replacing.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Dover

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Many water providers require yearly testing on irrigation and commercial backflow preventers, and our certified testers check each check valve and relief valve and file the results with your utility. We repair failures. Weather protection gets checked too. Late tests can mean fines.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Dover, NH

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. Roots, sags, cracks and grease show up on camera, especially in old clay and cast iron. You get a copy of the video. We lay out options if something needs work.

    Softener Upkeep in Dover, NH

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. We check the bypass and drain line. Then we test the treated water.

    Drain Camera Checks During Maintenance in Dover

    Sewer problems hide underground. The camera finds roots, grease, sags and offsets before they back up into the house. Frost heave and shifting rocky soil can damage older sewer laterals and shallow water lines. We show you the video. Early finds often mean a small repair rather than a whole new line.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Dover

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections with leak detector solution, make sure each appliance shutoff valve turns and replace any old or kinked flexible connectors. Repairs follow local code. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside.

    Sump Pump Testing in Dover, NH

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Nor'easters and tropical storm remnants can bring heavy rain that overwhelms drainage and floods low-lying areas. We fill the pit to test the float, check the discharge line and confirm the check valve holds. Backup pumps get tested too.
