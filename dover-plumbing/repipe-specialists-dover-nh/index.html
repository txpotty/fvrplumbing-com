    Repiping Work We Handle in Dover, NH

    Slab Leak Repiping in Dover

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Frost heave and shifting rocky soil can crack older sewer laterals and shallow water lines. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Dover

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Two or three days covers most houses.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Dover, NH

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Our crew cuts neat rectangles, covers nearby surfaces and patches each opening after the pressure test. Textures get matched. We match knockdown, orange peel or smooth walls, so the patches are ready to prime and paint.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Dover, NH

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. We make the house safe and usable first, with temporary repairs that get you water, then schedule the full repipe as soon as the crew and permit allow. We move fast. Even urgent repipes usually take just two to four days after we begin.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Dover

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. Rerouting one branch overhead and leaving the bad pipe in the slab is often cheaper than both a repipe and a jackhammer repair. It suits a first-time leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Dover

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Dover, NH

    A large share of homes rely on private wells, and some wells carry naturally occurring arsenic, radon or iron that call for treatment. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    PEX Repiping in Dover, NH

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. Flow stays strong at the last shower.
