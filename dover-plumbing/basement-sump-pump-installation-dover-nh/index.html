    Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise groundwater quickly in New Hampshire, sending water into basements that stay dry most of the year. Outages and storms arrive together, so we recommend a battery backup in most basements. The backup starts on its own if the main pump has no power, and it also catches the water if the primary switch sticks or the pump simply cannot keep up with a heavy inflow. Think of it as cheap insurance.

    Liberty Pumps, Zoeller and Wayne are our regulars. All three use cast iron housings and reliable switches, and all three have solid parts support through local supply houses, which matters more than a fancy feature list when something breaks. We match the model to your pit.

    Watch for constant running, rapid cycling or grinding noises. A sticking float or rust streaks on the housing also mean trouble, as is water sitting above the switch level without the pump starting. Do not wait for the storm.
