    Electric Tankless Water Heater Sizing and Selection in Dover

    Sizing comes down to two numbers, flow rate and temperature rise. If your groundwater comes in at 50 degrees and you want 120 at the shower, that is a 70 degree rise, and a 27 kW unit delivers about 2.6 gallons per minute at that rise. Warmer water means more flow. Panel and pipe layout matter as well.
