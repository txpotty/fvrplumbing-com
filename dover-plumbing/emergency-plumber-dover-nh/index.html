    Pinhole and Fitting Leaks in Dover

    Pressure above 80 PSI puts constant stress on every joint and valve. Aggressive water can also eat pinholes in copper, which start as a mist and open into a spray. We shut the water, cut out the bad section and replace it with copper or PEX, then put a gauge on a hose bib to read your static pressure. A failed regulator is common.

    Overflowing Toilets and Runaway Fixtures in Dover

    A faucet that will not shut off will soak a sink base quickly. Older multi-turn stops often seize, so the homeowner cannot close them. We replace the cartridge, fill valve or flapper that failed, and swap the stuck valve for a new quarter-turn stop under the sink. Next time, you can close it yourself.

    Severe Drain Clog Clearing in Dover, NH

    Grease, wipes and scale are behind the bulk of our emergency drain calls. Dishwasher backups are usually branch clogs. Water rising in the tub when the toilet flushes points to the main line, and that one needs a cleanout and a cable machine. We open it, flush it, then run a camera if it clogged before.

    Gas Line Emergency Services in Dover

    A patch of dead grass along the buried line can be a sign of a slow gas leak. So can bubbling in a puddle. Our licensed plumbers shut the supply, locate the leak with a detector and soap test, and replace the damaged pipe with approved material and fittings, then hold a pressure test for the time your local code requires. Pilots get relit only after the test passes.

    Gas Odor Calls in Dover, NH

    Gas smell inside means get out first. Call 911 or the gas utility once you are a safe distance away, and do not start a car in an attached garage.     Emergency Water Heater Repair in Dover

    No hot water at night is often a control problem, not a dead tank. On gas units, we check the thermocouple or flame sensor, the gas valve and the venting.
