    Commercial Water Heater Service in Dover, NH

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless units get a descaling flush. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Dover

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Most homes have basements, so foundation drainage and sump pumps matter for keeping the lower level dry. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.

    Grease Trap Installation in Dover
