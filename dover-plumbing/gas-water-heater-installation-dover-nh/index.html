    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Dover, NH

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Dover

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Propane runs at a different pressure and needs a smaller burner orifice, so we install propane-specific models or convert units with the manufacturer kit where the manual allows it. We work with your supplier. Every joint is leak tested.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Dover, NH

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. They need an outlet. Termination clearances follow the manual.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Dover, NH

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. That load usually calls for a 3/4 inch gas line or larger and a dedicated vent in PVC or stainless steel, depending on whether the unit is condensing. They never run out. Testing well water around Dover is the right first step before choosing a neutralizer or filter. A scale filter may be worth adding.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Dover

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them. We install their Defender models to the manual, keeping the bottom air intake clear and the clearances right. Thick steel, good parts. Every one goes in to code.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Dover

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. Plan on a full day. Our licensed plumbers handle all of it to code.

    Brands We Install in Dover

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith, Navien and Rinnai make up most of the gas heaters we install in Dover homes and businesses. Each brand has different warranty lengths, recovery rates and vent options, and we lay those out side by side so you can pick based on facts. Price is part of it. We install all of them.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in Dover, NH

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We figure your household peak demand from bathrooms and people, then make sure the vent diameter, rise and total length match the manufacturer table and local code. A small vent can backdraft. We test it every time.
