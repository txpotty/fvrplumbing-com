    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Dover

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Leak Service in Dover

    A broken stem can leave water running nonstop. Use the stops behind the access panel or the main shutoff, then open a low faucet to bleed pressure. We prioritize water that will not stop. Most get fixed on the first visit.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Dover, NH

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Dover, NH

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Dover, NH

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Dover, NH

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.
