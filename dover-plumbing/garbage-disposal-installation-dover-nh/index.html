    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Dover

    On a batch-feed unit, the lid is the switch.     Commercial Disposal Setup in Dover

    Restaurants and commercial kitchens in Dover need disposals built for constant use. These units usually require a dedicated circuit, a larger drain connection and a control panel that runs water automatically, and some sewer districts limit or ban them, so we check first. Downtime costs money.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Dover

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit is bigger and heavier than a starter model, so we measure the cabinet first.
