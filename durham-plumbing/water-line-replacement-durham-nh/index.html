    Water Line Replacement Work in Durham, NH

    Copper Service Line Installation in Durham, NH

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pack sand around it so rocks do not touch the pipe. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Durham, NH

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Durham

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe. After a second leak in a new spot, we stop recommending patches. We dig at both ends, pull or cut out the old pipe, and run new line at the depth local code calls for, then tie into the meter and the house valve. Most homes are done in a day.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Durham

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Durham

    We start at the meter. If the little dial on the meter is spinning with every fixture off, water is leaving somewhere. From there we listen along the line with an acoustic ground mic, check the house side for a toilet or softener running, and sometimes push a camera into the line when we suspect root damage or a crushed section, so the hole goes in the right spot.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Durham, NH

    Commercial services run bigger, often 1-1/2 to 2 inches, and almost always have a backflow assembly that needs testing after any work. Shutdowns get scheduled for when you are closed. For a restaurant or an office building that might mean an early morning start, temporary water to the restrooms, and a call to the utility about their side of the meter. Tenants get notice.

    Commercial Water Service Replacement in Durham

    Strip centers, churches and small apartment buildings each come with their own wrinkles. Some have a shared meter feeding several units. We map where each branch goes before cutting anything, coordinate with the utility for any work at the meter, and handle the permit, backflow test and inspection so the owner gets one point of contact. Paperwork included.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Durham

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Water Line Replacement Near Durham, NH

    Nearby neighbors often have the same pipe you do, since a whole street was usually built at once. Same builder, same pipe. If one house has failing poly, the next one probably does too. We see that pattern in Durham and can usually tell you what we expect to find before we start digging.

    Being local means we know how service lines in Durham are usually laid out. Some run straight from the street, others jog around old additions. That helps us price trenchless work honestly, guess the depth of the old pipe before we dig, and pick a route that saves the trees. Short drive, long memory.
