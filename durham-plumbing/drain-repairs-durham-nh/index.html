    Restaurant and Office Drain Repair in Durham

    Restaurants, salons, offices and medical suites all need working drains during business hours. Grease is the big one. We repair grease-laden kitchen lines, floor sinks, mop sink drains and restroom fixtures, working before opening or after close to keep the doors open. Maintenance plans catch trouble early.

    Sewer Drain Line Repair in Durham

    Every drain in the house empties into one building drain. Trouble there affects everything. Cracks, offsets and collapses in that line cause whole-house backups, so we use a liner when the pipe allows it and a targeted excavation when it does not. The camera tells us which.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Durham

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Find it and fix it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and each one shows up clearly on a camera or when we pull the trap. The fix goes where the clog starts.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Durham

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Remodels cause a lot of these. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, while fixtures that never had a vent can sometimes get an air admittance valve if code allows. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Durham, NH

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then cut out the damaged section and replace it with matching PVC, ABS or cast iron. Repairs meet local plumbing code.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Durham, NH

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. Most homes have basements, so foundation drainage and sump systems matter for keeping the lower level dry. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, and we locate the bad section on camera before replacing it with wrapped perforated pipe in washed stone. The outlet gets checked as well.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in Durham

    Downspout lines and yard drains fail without much warning. Water pools at the house. We clear the line, camera it for crushed or disconnected sections, and repair broken runs so downspout water gets carried away from the footing. We check the grade at the outlet too.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Durham, NH

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.
