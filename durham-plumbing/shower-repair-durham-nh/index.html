    Shower Drain Body Repair in Durham

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. The drain gets cleared and tested. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Durham, NH

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Durham, NH

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.
