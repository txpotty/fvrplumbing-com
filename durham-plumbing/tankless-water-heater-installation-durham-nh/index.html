    Conversions in Durham tend to run a full workday. We drain and remove the tank, upsize and test the gas, run a new vent through a wall or roof, then mount, plumb and fire the unit with service valves. Hot water is back by evening. The inspector comes after.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Durham, NH

    Businesses in Durham, NH with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank.     Cost-Effective Tankless Conversions in Durham, NH

    A tankless conversion in Durham, NH costs more than a tank swap, mostly because of the gas line upgrade, the new venting and the unit price itself. The savings come back over time.     Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Durham

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. We weigh both. A far bath may need recirc.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Durham

    Gas tankless units in Durham are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Durham, NH

    Upfront, a tankless install in Durham, NH costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows. Lower standby loss, a lifespan of up to 20 years against about 10 for a tank, and parts that can be replaced instead of the whole unit all count toward the total cost of ownership. Ask for both prices.
