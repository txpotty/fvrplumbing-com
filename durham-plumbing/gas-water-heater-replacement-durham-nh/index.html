    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Durham

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, and the new one goes on a pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. We soap-test the gas joints and check the draft hood with the burner lit.

    No Hot Water Calls in Durham

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Durham

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. Sizing and gas hookup are part of the job. We confirm the vent meets their specs.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Durham, NH

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. More warranty years generally buy a heavier anode rod. Choosing is simple.

    Commercial Gas Tank Replacement in Durham

    Laundromats and multi-unit buildings work commercial tanks so hard that replacement comes on a predictable schedule rather than as a surprise. We plan the swap around your slow hours. Some jobs need added vent or gas work.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Durham

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. We disconnect the old unit and reconnect the new one to the existing gas line and flue. Closets need enough combustion air. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Durham

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Durham, NH

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. Bad vent sections and draft hoods get replaced. We check draft with a match or mirror at the hood.
