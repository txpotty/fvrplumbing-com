    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Durham, NH

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500.     Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Durham
