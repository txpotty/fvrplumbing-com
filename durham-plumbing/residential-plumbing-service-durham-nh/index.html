    Some jobs are fine to DIY. A flapper swap or a trap cleanout is a reasonable weekend task, but gas lines, water heaters and anything inside a wall can turn into a flood, a fire risk or a failed inspection if the work is off. A licensed plumber gets those right and to code.

    Emergencies go to the top of the board. We aim to have a plumber at the house within a few hours, and for a burst pipe or sewage backup we will walk you through closing the main shutoff on the phone so the damage stops while we drive. Grab towels and a bucket while you wait.

    Watch for drips that will not stop, slow drains, banging or whistling pipes, a sudden drop in pressure, discolored water and wet spots with no obvious source. Each one is a symptom of something that will get worse. Do not ignore them.

    Insulate any pipe in an unheated space, like a crawlspace, attic or garage wall.     Every one to two years works for most homes in Durham, NH. The inspection usually runs about an hour, and we check pressure, look for slow leaks at shutoffs and supply lines, test the water heater relief valve and look for corrosion on exposed pipe. Problems found early stay cheap.
