    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap.     Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Durham, NH

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. The debris flushes away as normal waste, leaving nothing behind to harm pipe or septic bacteria.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Durham

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them.     Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Durham

    Price depends on the line size, the length and what is in it. Small lines cost less than a main. Most residential jetting runs a few hundred dollars, while restaurant grease lines and large commercial mains cost more because of the time, the larger equipment and the extra camera work they need. We quote before starting.

    A snake bores a hole through the clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. The snake leaves grease, scale and root fibers on the pipe walls where they catch the next clog, while jetting removes that buildup so the line stays clear far longer.

    Repeat clogs, slow drains throughout the house and gurgling toilets all point to buildup. So does a sewer smell. If a snake has cleared the same line more than once in a year, the pipe walls are probably coated, and jetting is the step that actually removes the coating instead of opening another temporary hole.

    Most home jetting jobs take one to three hours. That includes the camera work. Heavy commercial lines can take most of a day since we work in passes.

    It depends on the line. Home lines get a cart jetter. Sewer mains and commercial lines get a trailer jetter with more flow, since flow is what carries loosened debris out of a 4-inch or 6-inch pipe, while pressure is what breaks the buildup loose.

    We guarantee the line will flow freely when we leave. The service warranty is written on your invoice. If the camera shows a defect that could cause a clog to return, such as a belly or a root-filled crack, we point it out before we start so the terms are clear and nobody is surprised later.

    Clogs that return within weeks of snaking are the clearest sign. Several slow drains point the same way. Gurgling, sewer odor and kitchen backups suggest buildup a cable will not remove.
