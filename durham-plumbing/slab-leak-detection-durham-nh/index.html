    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Durham

    Pressure isolation is always the first step.     Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Durham, NH

    A second slab leak is often a sign of more. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. Most repipes wrap up in two to four days.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Durham

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Durham

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones.     Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Durham, NH

    Listening Through Concrete in Durham

    Ground microphones are sensitive. That means appliances, HVAC fans and traffic all show up in the headphones, so our techs shut off what they can and learn to tune out the rest, and that skill only comes with lots of practice. Pressurized lines are the easiest to hear in your Durham home.

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.
