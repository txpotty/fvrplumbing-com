    What Goes Into a PEX Install in Durham, NH

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Durham, NH

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines. While the studs are exposed we can run PEX back to a manifold or a sound trunk, set fresh stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a fridge line. Additions get their own sizing.     New Construction PEX Plumbing in Durham, NH

    On a new build, FVR Plumbing runs PEX supply lines while the framing is still open in Durham, NH. We drill the plates, set the manifold and stub out every fixture to the heights on the plans, then hold a pressure test for the rough-in inspection. Steel nail plates cover every stud crossing. It keeps the schedule moving.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Durham

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. If the shower valve sits two inches low or the vanity stubs miss the cabinet, the wall gets reopened after tile, and that costs time and money. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. Then we pressure test with the walls open.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Durham

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Durham, NH

    A single bad section does not always mean a repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. That works for one leak. By the third leak, though, we will talk to you honestly about whether the rest of that copper is close behind.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Installation in Durham

    Commercial PEX work in Durham runs on the business schedule, not ours. For a small office, a restaurant restroom or a retail tenant space, we can stage the work after closing so the water is only off when nobody needs it. Bigger sizes join the same way. Larger jobs may call for one-inch or bigger trunk lines and commercial-grade manifolds.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Durham, NH

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.
