    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Durham, NH

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Durham

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning.     Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Durham

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Durham, NH

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. If you bought a Pelican system for your home, we install it with secure connections, a proper bypass and the startup steps the manual lists for flushing the media. Startup matters. Every step gets done.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Durham

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.
