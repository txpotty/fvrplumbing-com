    Leaking Toilet Repair in Durham

    Water on the floor can come from four places: the wax ring, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the supply line or sweat running off a cold tank. Each one needs a different fix. We dry everything and watch where the water shows up first, which takes about ten minutes and saves pulling a toilet that did not need it. If the porcelain itself is cracked, we price a new bowl rather than trying to seal it.

    Toilet Leaking Through the Ceiling in Durham, NH

    Water coming through the ceiling under an upstairs bathroom usually traces back to a failed wax ring or a cracked supply nut, and the drywall below holds the evidence. Leave that toilet alone until we look. The toilet comes up, the flange gets checked, and the bowl goes back on a new seal, then we show you how far the water traveled so you can decide about the ceiling.

    Toilet Brands We Service in Durham

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield make up most of the toilets we open, and each one uses its own flapper size or flush valve design. The wrong flapper will leak. We match the part to the model number stamped inside the tank and stock the common ones on every truck.

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Durham, NH

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We bring the water up to the line stamped on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A cracked handle arm or a stripped mounting nut takes about five minutes to replace.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Durham, NH

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. When the auger finds nothing and the bowl still fills, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. We run a cable from the cleanout or put a camera in.

    Emergency Toilet Repair in Durham, NH

    An overflowing bowl in a one-bathroom house is an emergency, and so is a burst supply line spraying water across the floor at full house pressure. Shut the angle stop first. Turn the valve on the wall behind the toilet clockwise until it is tight, then call us so we can walk you through the rest. We stop the leak on the first visit and fix it for good when parts allow.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Durham, NH

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. A stiff gasket stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. We replace the whole set of tank seals at once when one is gone.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Durham

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper. The level falls a little until the fill valve opens back up. Other times the refill tube sits too deep in the overflow pipe, which siphons the tank. We clip it above the water line.
