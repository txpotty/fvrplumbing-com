    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Durham

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. We test that first. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. You will know which before we quote a pump.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Durham

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. Then a new check valve goes on. Skip that part and the pump short cycles.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Durham, NH

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (844) 442-1569 and we will send someone. Keep the lid off, unplug anything sitting on the wet floor, and use a wet vac to hold the water below the rim until we get there. Common pumps ride on the truck, so most swaps happen right then. We will also figure out what killed the old pump.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Durham, NH

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. We break out the concrete, set a larger poly basin with a sealed lid, and pour the floor back. More volume in the pit means fewer starts per hour. Fewer starts, longer motor life.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Durham

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Durham, NH

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Grinder and ejector pumps are a separate job.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Durham

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. We replace it with the right Wayne or another cast iron unit. Takes about two hours.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Durham, NH

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule.
