    Swapping Out Old Disposals the Right Way in Durham, NH

    Standard Garbage Disposal Replacement in Durham

    Every swap starts with the power off. We disconnect the dishwasher hose and the discharge tube, turn the mounting ring to drop the old unit, then pull the old flange and clean off the dried putty so the new flange seats flat in the sink opening.     Commercial Unit Replacement in Durham

    Restaurant disposals take a beating every shift, and when one fails the dish line backs up. We replace failed commercial units with heavy-duty models sized to your volume, check the control panel and water solenoid, and confirm the setup meets local health and sewer rules. Local rules vary.

    Waste King and Moen Replacement in Durham, NH

    Waste King units hang on an EZ Mount ring, which twists on by hand, and many Moen units use a similar style. We match the replacement to the mount when it makes sense, or replace the flange and mount when switching brands, so the new disposal hangs tight with no vibration at the sink. Brand changes take a little longer. A new flange goes in.

    Like-for-Like Disposal Swap in Durham

    If the replacement uses the same mount as the old unit, the swap is quick and the trap usually lines up without changes. We still replace the flange gasket and putty if they have hardened, and we check the discharge tube for cracks, since old plastic tubes split right at the gasket. Figure on an hour. We haul the old one away.

    Same-Day Disposal Replacement in Durham, NH

    A failed disposal with a sink full of water cannot wait a week. Our trucks carry common 1/2 and 3/4 HP units, mounts and cord kits, so in most cases we diagnose, replace and test on the same visit without a trip to the supply house. Morning calls usually get afternoon slots.

    Mount and Flange Replacement in Durham

    During every replacement we inspect the sink flange and mount. Corroded, scratched or leaking parts get replaced, which stops top leaks and gives the new disposal a tight seal at the sink. We clean off the old putty.

    Leaking Disposal Replacement in Durham

    Water from the bottom of the housing means the internal seal has failed, and that seal is not a replaceable part. We pull the unit, dry the cabinet, check the floor for soft spots and install a new disposal with a new flange and gaskets so every seal on the assembly is fresh. Swollen cabinet bases need repair. We point it out before it gets worse.

    Upgrading During Replacement in Durham, NH

    A swap is the right moment to move up in horsepower, from an old 1/3 HP to a 1/2 or 3/4 HP unit. Larger units are taller, so we measure the cabinet and check that the discharge outlet will still sit above the trap, since a disposal outlet below the wall stub leaves water standing in the chamber. Insulated models run much quieter. Most people notice it the first night.
