    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Durham

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Those get heat cable. Old-style constant-wattage heat tape can overheat and is a known fire risk, so we install self-regulating cable listed for the pipe material and replace worn or brittle heat tape we find.

    Attic and Exterior Wall Pipe Repair in Durham

    Many homes run supply lines through attics or crawlspaces to reach bathrooms and kitchens. Those are the first to freeze. After repairing a split, we look at whether the line can be moved into a heated space, buried under attic insulation on the warm side, or wrapped with heat cable, since the same pipe will freeze again otherwise.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Durham, NH

    Water expands as it freezes, and that pressure can split copper, crack PVC fittings and pop PEX off a crimp ring. We replace the damaged section. Where one split appears, others often follow along the same run, so we pressure test the full branch and replace any section that bulged or thinned instead of patching only the obvious leak.

    Freeze Protection for Commercial Buildings in Durham, NH

    Vacant suites and warehouses freeze when the heat gets turned down too far. Property managers get a freeze plan. That plan lists the shutoffs, the at-risk runs, the heat cable and the minimum thermostat setting for each space, so building staff know exactly what to do when a cold front is in the forecast.

    Water Heater Line Thawing and Repair in Durham

    Water heaters in garages or attics sit in spaces where the lines can freeze. Tankless units are especially vulnerable. Many tankless models have built-in freeze protection that only works with power on, so after an outage we check the heat exchanger and drain valves, and we replace any cracked flex connectors on tank heaters.

    Slab Leak Detection for Frozen Pipes in Durham

    Pipes under a slab rarely freeze, but the lines where they rise out of the slab at an exterior wall can. That riser is the weak spot. Acoustic and thermal tools find slab-area leaks without breaking up the floor first.

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and take the coldest air directly. Brass bodies crack. We repair or replace the damaged assembly, have it tested if your water utility requires it, and show you how to drain it or fit an insulated cover before the next freeze.
