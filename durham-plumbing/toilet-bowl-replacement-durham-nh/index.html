    Cracked Bolt Hole Repair in Durham

    Hairline cracks near the bolt holes often start when someone tightens the closet bolts too far, and they spread a little with every flush and every sit. Epoxy will not hold. We replace the bowl and use bolts snugged by hand, then finish with a quarter turn so the porcelain is not stressed again.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Durham

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. The bolt holes get a close look before we quote. For a worn gasket, a new bolt kit fixes it, and a cracked bolt hole means a new bowl with stainless or brass hardware.

    Cost-Effective Bowl Swaps in Durham, NH

    Swapping only the bowl saves money when the tank is newer, the fill valve and flush valve are in good shape, and the bowl is still made in your color. White is the easiest to match. Discontinued colors are where bowl-only swaps fall apart, and then a full toilet costs about the same.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Remodels in Durham

    Remodels are a good time to change the bowl shape or height, as long as the rough-in stays the same and the new footprint covers the old flooring cutout. We check that before you order. We work alongside your contractor or tile setter so the flange ends up at the right height above the finished floor.

    Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in Durham

    Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, camps and seasonal homes are especially likely to freeze when the heat is turned down. Water left sitting in a toilet in an unheated cabin or vacant house can freeze, and the ice cracks the bowl at the trap where you cannot see it. Toilets in unheated spaces should be drained. We replace the cracked bowl and show you how to winterize, which means shutting the stop, flushing and adding RV antifreeze to the bowl and tank.

    Emergency Bowl Replacement in Durham

    Shut the angle stop behind the toilet the moment you see water coming from a cracked bowl, then pull towels around the base to slow the spread. Then call us.
