    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Durham

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. Stuck floats, worn motors and failed check valves cause most of the service calls we run on sumps. We test yours with a bucket. Add a battery backup if storms knock out power.

    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Durham

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. We use a cast iron pump in most homes and a 1-1/2 inch discharge with a check valve. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It usually ends outside, well away from the foundation, at a spot your local rules allow.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Durham

    Commercial basements hold inventory, records or mechanical rooms that cannot sit in water. Elevator pits, floor drains and sumps all get checked together because one weak link floods the floor. Duplex pumps help. Alarms on the pit tell staff before water reaches the floor.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Durham

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Most homes have basements, so foundation drainage and sump systems matter for keeping the lower level dry. Before anyone cuts the floor, we check the gutters, downspout extensions and the grade along that wall, because fixing those outside often stops seepage for a fraction of the cost. Cheap fixes come first.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Durham, NH

    Damp walls without a crack usually mean water moving through porous concrete or block. Tape foil to the wall for a full day, and if the room side is wet it is condensation, not a leak. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Real seepage calls for better drainage or an interior channel.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Durham

    Water lines along a basement ceiling tend to leak at fittings, valves and pinholes. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Durham, NH

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Expect a few days of work. We bring in a waterproofing partner for the membrane and handle the footing drain and its tie-in to a sump or daylight outlet ourselves.
