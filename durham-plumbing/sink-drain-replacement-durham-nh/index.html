    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Durham

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain. We pull the old body, clean the flange seat down to bare porcelain, reset the new flange in putty or silicone, and adjust the clevis so the lift rod opens and closes the stopper with one easy motion. We match the faucet finish. Overflow-style drains are standard.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Durham, NH

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. A press on the cap opens and closes them, and a slotted body only goes in sinks with an overflow. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    P-Trap Replacement in Durham

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust from the inside, the plating flakes off, and a slip nut that will not loosen often twists the whole trap apart, so we cut it out and install heavy-wall PVC or polished brass that matches the room. The new trap gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Durham, NH

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Durham

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. Mismatched drains are common. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Durham

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. We replace the strainer and trap together. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Durham

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. Sinks below the sewer line drain through a pump, so we check its valve and discharge line during the job. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in Durham

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The disposal outlet must sit above the trap, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or air gap, and nothing can pull sideways. We set them all at once. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.
