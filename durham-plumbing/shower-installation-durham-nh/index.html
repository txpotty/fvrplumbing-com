    How We Install Showers in Durham, NH

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Durham

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Durham

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Testing follows. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Shower Stall Installation in Durham

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. The drain connects from below. Flow and temperature get tested after trim.

    Shower Pan Installation in Durham

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. We level prefab pans carefully. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Durham, NH

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. Thresholds stay low or flat. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Durham, NH

    Budget showers can still be built right. A one-piece unit with a good valve is a fraction of the cost of tile, and set properly on mortar it holds up for a long time. The valve is where to spend. You get the full price before we start.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Durham, NH

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Digital Shower Systems in Durham, NH

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. An electrician handles the outlet. Our part is the plumbing.
