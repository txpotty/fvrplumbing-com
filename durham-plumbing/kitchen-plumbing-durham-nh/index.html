    Replacing an Old Kitchen Sink in Durham

    Pulling an old cast iron sink takes two people and a putty knife, since they are heavy and usually caulked in tight. We check the cutout first.     Grease Trap Cleaning in Durham

    A grease trap that smells sour is usually past due for cleaning. We remove the lid, scrape the baffles, pull the floating grease and settled solids, and check the gasket so the lid seals again instead of venting into the kitchen. Each cleaning goes in the log. Inspectors usually want to see that log before anything else.

    New Disposal Installation in Durham

    Adding a disposal where there never was one means a switched outlet or circuit in the cabinet, and that part is electrical work. We handle the plumbing and can line up the electrician. Because the disposal outlet is lower than the old tailpiece, the trap usually has to come down an inch or two, and the wall stub has to allow it.

    Recurring Kitchen Clogs in Durham, NH

    Grease, rice, pasta and coffee grounds build up in the trap and branch line over months, not days. We clear the line with a cable sized for kitchen pipe and can camera it when the same clog returns. Sometimes the pipe itself is the problem. A low spot in the line or a drain arm without enough fall will collect grease however often it is cleared.

    Disposal Repair and Replacement in Durham, NH

    A disposal that hums but will not spin usually has something wedged in it. We cut the power, work the flywheel loose with a 1/4 inch hex key from underneath, pull out the bottle cap or bone, and press the red reset button before testing it again. Leaking units are another matter. A drip from the bottom of the housing means the internal seal failed, and that unit has to be replaced.

    InSinkErator Installation in Durham, NH

    Many homeowners ask to step up from an older InSinkErator to an Evolution because the newer units are quieter and grind finer. Often the mount stays. When the old Quick Lock mount is sound, we can reuse it, which saves time and leaves the sink flange alone, and we register the warranty for you.

    Workstation and Farmhouse Sinks in Durham

    An apron-front sink needs cabinet support built to hold it, since a 30 inch fireclay bowl can weigh over 100 pounds before water. The support frame gets checked first. Many workstation sinks have a drain set off to the back or side, so we plan the trap and disposal layout around it before the counter crew arrives.

    Burst Supply Lines in Durham, NH

    Plastic supply lines with plastic nuts crack more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. A failed line under the sink can put gallons per minute into the cabinet and the floor below before anyone notices it. Both supply lines get replaced. If one line cracked from age, the other one is about the same age.
