    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Durham, NH

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is finished level with the floor.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Durham, NH

    A spot repair costs least up front. Then add the flooring. Reroutes cost somewhat more but skip the concrete work and prevent future leaks in that line, and a whole-house repipe makes sense once you have had several slab leaks or have old copper throughout. You get clear prices for each.

    Slab Leak Detection in Durham

    The first test is the water meter. We shut off everything first. If the meter still turns, we close the water heater inlet and pressure test hot and cold separately, then walk the losing line with a ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Commercial Slab Leak Repair in Durham, NH

    Commercial slabs in Durham carry longer pipe runs and more branches. It means more line to test. We isolate branches one at a time, locate the leak and schedule cutting for after hours or a weekend, so your doors stay open and your customers never see a saw. Work meets New Hampshire code and stays safe and compliant.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Durham

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Durham home.

    Slab Leaks in Stores and Offices in Durham

    Retail stores, offices and restaurants on slabs develop the same leaks as houses. Tenants feel it fast. Leaks get found and fixed around business hours, usually after closing, and you get a written report with photos for managers and insurers.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Durham, NH

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. A repipe may be cheaper long term. PEX flexes with the house, does not corrode and can be run through the attic and walls, so a full repipe replaces every supply line and takes the slab out of the picture. It suits many Durham homes.
